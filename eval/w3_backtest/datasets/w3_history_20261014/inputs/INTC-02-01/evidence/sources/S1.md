## Cutoff-eligible evidence summary

Signal65 compares three retail 14-inch systems: Lenovo Slim 7x with standard 18-core Snapdragon X2 Elite X2E-88-100, Dell XPS 14 with Intel Core Ultra X7 358H, and Lenovo Yoga 7a 14 with AMD Ryzen AI 7 445. It reports Snapdragon leads over the Intel system of 31% in Geekbench 7 multicore and 24% single-core, and 97% versus Intel's 95% average performance retention across six battery benchmarks. The report also records Intel wins in integrated graphics and sustained Office battery runtime. It describes whole-system comparisons and flags configuration differences; Windows Central identifies Qualcomm as the study commissioner.
