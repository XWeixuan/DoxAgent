## Cutoff-eligible evidence summary

Intel reports Xeon 6-based instances delivered higher normalized task throughput than the tested 5th Gen AMD EPYC and Arm v9.2A instances across healthcare, banking/financial services, and manufacturing tasks. It describes a deterministic Terminal-Bench 2.0 replay, 24-way concurrency, a 60-minute window, and a whole-box tasks-per-60-minutes metric. Intel identifies the configurations as AWS instances with 48 vCPU and 185 GB memory and says testing was in September 2026.
