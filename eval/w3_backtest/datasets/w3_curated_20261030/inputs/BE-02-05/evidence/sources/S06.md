# Meta seeks new data center financing

Publisher: Bloomberg via Advisor Perspectives
URL: https://www.advisorperspectives.com/articles/2026/05/05/meta-taps-morgan-stanley-jpmorgan-new-deal?topic=income
Published: 2026-05-05
Role: prior AI financing

Bloomberg reported Meta was arranging a roughly $13 billion financing package for an El Paso data center, mostly expected to be debt, with discussions and terms still fluid. It described the earlier Louisiana deal as nearly $30 billion including $27 billion debt through Beignet and noted investors were funding AI expansion through corporate and project bonds.
