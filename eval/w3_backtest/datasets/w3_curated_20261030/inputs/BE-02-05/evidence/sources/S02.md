# Oracle sends force majeure notice on New Mexico data center

Publisher: TechCrunch
URL: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/oracle-sends-force-majeure-notice-on-its-new-mexico-stargate-data-center/
Published: 2026-09-24T11:11:00-07:00
Role: project context and responses

Oracle sent the notice to developer Stack Infrastructure; Bloomberg sources said it was not seeking to exit as principal tenant and the notice would permit delayed payments if the project misses its 2028 target. Oracle said it expected no delay, and Blue Owl said the notice did not change financial commitments. The project was designed for 2.45 GW and Bloom gas fuel cells; its gas pipeline had been delayed to February 2027 and an air permit was pending.
