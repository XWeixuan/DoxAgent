# Meta announces Hyperion joint venture

Publisher: Meta
URL: https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2025/Meta-Announces-Joint-Venture-with-Funds-Managed-by-Blue-Owl-Capital-to-Develop-Hyperion-Data-Center/
Published: 2025-10-21
Role: primary financing terms

Meta and Blue Owl funds announced a joint venture to develop and own Hyperion, with Blue Owl funds owning 80% and Meta 20%. Parties committed to fund their pro rata share of approximately $27 billion of development costs. Meta leased all campus facilities for an initial four years with extension options and provided a capped residual value guarantee for the first 16 operating years if specified conditions arise on lease nonrenewal or termination. Blue Owl raised some capital through debt in a private offering.
