# Global Debt Report 2026

Publisher: OECD
URL: https://www.sipotra.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Global-Debt-Report-2026.pdf
Published: 2026-04
Role: prior financing structure

The OECD describes an October 2025 Meta and Blue Owl affiliated funds joint venture with approximately $29 billion committed funding. Blue Owl affiliates contributed $23 billion for 80% and Meta subsidiary Iris Crossing $6 billion for 20%. A Blue Owl subsidiary issued $27 billion in Beignet debt. Repayment was expected to start upon campus completion in 2029 through 2049. Meta residual value guarantees can reach $28 billion and are not recorded on its balance sheet.
