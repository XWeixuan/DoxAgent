# Are AI credit cracks a warning, or a buy signal?

Publisher: Reuters
URL: https://www.reuters.com/commentary/reuters-open-interest/are-ai-credit-cracks-warning-or-buy-signal-2026-09-29/
Published: 2026-09-29T09:02:00-04:00
Role: event column

Jamie McGeever describes Beignet Investor’s $27 billion senior secured bonds funding Meta’s Hyperion data center and states they traded at 91 cents on Monday, 7.55% yield and 230 basis points over Treasuries, compared with 185 at issuance and 255 in July. S&P rated them A+ and warned of substantial credit risk passing to Meta. The column describes Meta CDS approaching 100 basis points, AI spending and borrowing, and Oxford Economics’ return assumptions. It is commentary; it notes the underlying companies have strong businesses and are highly unlikely to default.
