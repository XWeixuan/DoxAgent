# Intel will keep working with Musk on Terafab venture

- URL: https://uk.marketscreener.com/news/intel-to-keep-working-on-musk-s-terafab-chipmaking-venture-bloomberg-news-reports-ce785dd9de8ef125
- 发布方: Reuters / MarketScreener
- 发布时间: 2026-10-07T06:12:00+01:00
- 角色: 同期采访报道

Reuters援引Bloomberg采访，Intel CEO陈立武表示继续与Musk在Terafab合作；报道同时指出外界此前讨论TSMC可能参与。该报道归因于采访，而非已签署的代工收入订单。
