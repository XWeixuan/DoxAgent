# Data center concerns and Oracle local response

- Publisher: Associated Press
- Published: 2026-08-26T04:01:58Z

AP reported local concerns about water resources and the scale of the planned $165 billion New Mexico complex. Oracle described jobs, tax effects and investment in local water and wastewater systems, and said its closed-loop cooling and fuel-cell systems were designed to use little water. The article reports differing claims rather than a regulatory decision.
