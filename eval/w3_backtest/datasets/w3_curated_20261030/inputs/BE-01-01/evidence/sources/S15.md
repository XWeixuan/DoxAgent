# Bloom 2025 annual report: customers and financing

- URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1664703/000162828026006516/be-20251231.htm
- Publisher: Bloom Energy / SEC
- Published: 2026-02
- Role: 销售与融资渠道

Bloom describes selling Energy Servers to financiers, strategic partners, distributors and direct end users. Its Brookfield financing framework allows up to $5 billion over five years for projects meeting investment and contractual criteria. Financing eligibility, a customer purchase and project commissioning are separate steps.
