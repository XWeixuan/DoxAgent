# Bloom and Equinix onsite power agreement

- URL: https://investor.bloomenergy.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2025/Bloom-Energy-Expands-Data-Center-Power-Agreement-with-Equinix-Surpassing-100MW/default.aspx
- Publisher: Bloom Energy / Equinix
- Published: 2025-02-20

In February 2025 Bloom and Equinix described more than 100 MW in their data-center relationship, comprising approximately 75 MW operating and 30 MW under construction. The systems supplemented grid power at 19 centers in six states, an independent customer relationship from Oracle.
