# Oracle pipeline rerouted after rejection

- URL: https://www.energyconnects.com/news/gas-lng/2026/august/oracle-data-center-s-pipeline-plan-is-rerouted-after-rejections/
- Publisher: Bloomberg / Energy Connects
- Published: 2026-08-18
- Role: 管线路由替代与工期

Energy Transfer subsidiary Transwestern proposed an alternate 17.8-mile natural gas route avoiding state trust land after the State Land Office twice rejected rights of way. The pipeline’s expected in-service date had moved from August 15, 2026 to February 1, 2027. Oracle said the data-center project remained on schedule.
