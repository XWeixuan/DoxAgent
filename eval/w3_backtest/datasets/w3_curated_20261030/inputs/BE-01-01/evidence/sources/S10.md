# Oracle statement on Jupiter construction permitting

- Publisher: Oracle
- Published: 2026-09-14
- Role: 建设与许可回应

Oracle stated that the air permit application concerns the microgrid, comprising fuel cells and related equipment, and that the data center buildings are separate facilities at an adjacent site. It said YGI was following New Mexico Environment Department guidance on permitted preconstruction while the air permit was pending.
