# Bloomberg: gas pipeline to Oracle project delayed

- URL: https://www.energyconnects.com/news/gas-lng/2026/august/gas-pipeline-for-proposed-oracle-data-center-delayed-to-2027/
- Publisher: Bloomberg / Energy Connects
- Published: 2026-08-14
- Role: 燃气供应与原回应

Transwestern revised the Green Chile pipeline’s projected in-service date to February 1, 2027 from August 15, 2026. Bloomberg reported a capacity of up to 400 million cubic feet of gas per day. An Energy Aspects analyst expected second-half 2027 at the earliest and cited cancellation risk; Oracle said the project remained on schedule.
