# New Mexico Supreme Court temporarily halts permit proceedings

- URL: https://sourcenm.com/2026/08/24/nm-supreme-court-temporarily-halt-project-jupiter-air-quality-permit-hearing/
- Publisher: Source New Mexico
- Published: 2026-08-24
- Role: 许可进程历史

The court temporarily halted the proposed gas-powered fuel-cell microgrid air-permit hearing and paused use of water from a specified well for construction. The subsequent September 17 court order lifted those stays; the two steps concern the legal process, not approval of the air permit.
