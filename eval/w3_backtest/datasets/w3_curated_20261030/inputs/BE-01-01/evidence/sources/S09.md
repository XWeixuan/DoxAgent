# New Mexico Supreme Court orders in Jupiter permit and water cases

- URL: https://nmcourts.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Orders-in-Project-Jupiter-air-quality-permit-and-water-cases.pdf
- Publisher: New Mexico Supreme Court
- Published: 2026-09-17
- Role: 许可诉讼当前状态

The court denied the petitions for writs of mandamus and lifted the August 23 stays in the air permit and water proceedings. Lifting those stays permits the proceedings to continue; the order does not itself issue an air permit or guarantee project completion.
