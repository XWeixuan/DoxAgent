# Oracle Announces Q1 FY2027 Results

- URL: https://investor.oracle.com/investor-news/news-details/2026/Oracle-Announces-Q1-Results-Driven-by-Triple-Digit-Growth-in-Cloud-Infrastructure-Revenues/
- Publisher: Oracle
- Published: 2026-09-10
- Role: 客户业务规模

Oracle reported fiscal Q1 2027 infrastructure cloud revenue of $7.4 billion, up 121% year over year; cloud application revenue was $4.2 billion. Oracle said it delivered 850 MW of additional data center capacity and that AI training and inference demand continued to grow faster than supply. These figures describe Oracle’s broader infrastructure business, not Intel processor orders.
