# Intel Reports Second-Quarter 2026 Financial Results

- URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/50863/000005086326000155/q226earningsrelease.htm
- Publisher: Intel / SEC
- Published: 2026-07-23
- Role: 业务基数和指引

Intel reported $16.1 billion in second-quarter revenue, up 25%, and $6.3 billion of data center and AI revenue, up 59%. It forecast third-quarter company revenue of $15.8–16.8 billion. DCAI includes server and other products and is not equivalent to a single cloud customer’s purchases.
