# Intel 2026 second quarter Form 10-Q

- URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/50863/000005086326000157/intc-20260627.htm
- Publisher: Intel / SEC
- Published: 2026-07-24
- Role: 供给与盈利基数

Intel reports Q2 DCAI revenue increased $2.3 billion year over year, driven largely by server revenue, and server average selling price rose 48%, mostly due to a more premium product mix. Server unit volume rose 9%, primarily from hyperscaler demand. Intel said internal capacity constrained supply and industry shortages of substrates, memory and other components could persist into the following year.
