# Snowflake FY2027 Q2 earnings release: operating metrics

- URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1640147/000164014726000033/fy2027q2earnings.htm
- Publisher: Snowflake / SEC
- Published: 2026-09-02
- Role: 财务与消费基线

Fiscal Q2 total revenue was $1.55 billion, product revenue $1.49 billion, net revenue retention 126%, and remaining performance obligations $9.00 billion, up 30%. The company had 828 customers with over $1 million in trailing twelve-month product revenue, and added 692 net new customers. Management cited a third consecutive quarter of accelerating product revenue growth and raised its full-year growth outlook to 36%. These metrics differ in their time periods and definitions.
