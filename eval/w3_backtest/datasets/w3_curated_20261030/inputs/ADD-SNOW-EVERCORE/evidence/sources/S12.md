# Guggenheim reiterates Neutral on Snowflake

- URL: https://ca.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/guggenheim-reiterates-neutral-rating-on-snowflake-stock-93CH-4827316
- Publisher: Investing.com
- Published: 2026-09-03
- Role: 其他分析师反向评价

Guggenheim analyst John DiFucci acknowledged strong results, faster migrations and the consumption model’s earlier revenue recognition. He said the outlook supported continued strength for the rest of the year, but maintained Neutral and cited valuation at about 18 times enterprise value to next-twelve-months recurring revenue. This was a distinct contemporaneous view.
