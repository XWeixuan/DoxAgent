# Snowflake raises FY2027 forecast after AI demand

- URL: https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/snowflake-lifts-annual-product-revenue-forecast-on-demand-for-cloud-data-platform-4886702
- Publisher: Reuters
- Published: 2026-09-02
- Role: 业绩与市场原预期

Reuters reported $1.55 billion of quarterly revenue against an LSEG analyst average of $1.48 billion and adjusted EPS $0.62 against $0.45 estimated. The CEO said AI products accounted for approximately half the acceleration; the company raised annual product revenue guidance to $6.07 billion. D.A. Davidson cited customer growth, backlog and operating leverage.
