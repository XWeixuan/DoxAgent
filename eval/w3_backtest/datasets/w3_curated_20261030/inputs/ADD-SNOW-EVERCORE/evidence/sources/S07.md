# Snowflake fiscal 2026 annual report: consumption and competition

- URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1640147/000164014726000023/snowflake2026ars.pdf
- Publisher: Snowflake / SEC
- Published: 2026-05-18
- Role: 收入模式与竞争

Snowflake generally recognizes revenue based on consumption rather than ratably like a subscription. Its annual report identifies public clouds AWS, Azure and Google Cloud as competitors alongside other data and observability companies. It says customer-controlled open formats can reduce lock-in, while expansion into AI and transactional products increases the number of competitors and customer requirements. The company recorded a fiscal 2026 net loss of $1.3 billion.
