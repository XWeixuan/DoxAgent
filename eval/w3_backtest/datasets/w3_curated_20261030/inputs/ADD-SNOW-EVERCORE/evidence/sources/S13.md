# RBC raises Snowflake target on AI growth

- URL: https://www.investing.com/news/analyst-ratings/rbc-capital-raises-snowflake-stock-price-target-on-ai-growth-93CH-4888102
- Publisher: Investing.com
- Published: 2026-09-03
- Role: 其他分析师先前增长路径

RBC raised its target from $372 to $440 and retained Outperform. It highlighted product revenue growth accelerating from 30% in fiscal Q4 2026 to 34% in fiscal Q1 and 37% in fiscal Q2 2027, and described potential further acceleration. The report attributed the target change to higher estimates and peer multiple expansion.
