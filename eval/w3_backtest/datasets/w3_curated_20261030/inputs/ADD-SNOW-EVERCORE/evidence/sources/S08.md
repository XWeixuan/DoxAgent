# Stifel raises Snowflake target after Q2 results

- URL: https://www.investing.com/news/analyst-ratings/stifel-raises-snowflake-stock-price-target-to-450-on-ai-growth-93CH-4887629
- Publisher: Investing.com
- Published: 2026-09-03T07:40:00-04:00
- Role: 分析师先前观点

Stifel raised its target to $450 and expected sustained annual product revenue growth above 30% over coming years, versus its prior expectation above 25%, citing AI and machine learning products and associated core consumption. The report says management reduced FY2027 product gross margin guidance to 74% due to higher AI mix while aiming to offset growing cloud costs with slower headcount expense. Guggenheim maintained a Neutral rating despite acknowledging strong results.
