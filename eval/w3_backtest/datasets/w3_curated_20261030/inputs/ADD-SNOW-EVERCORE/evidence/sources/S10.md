# SNOW Q2 Earnings Call Highlights AI Flywheel, Higher Outlook

- URL: https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/2984416/snow-q2-earnings-call-highlights-ai-flywheel-higher-outlook
- Publisher: Zacks
- Published: 2026-09-03
- Role: 财报电话会与先前增长

Zacks reported that Snowflake management linked the higher fiscal 2027 product revenue outlook to core platform consumption and AI adoption; guidance was $6.07 billion, or 36% year-over-year growth. Fiscal Q3 product revenue guidance was $1.588–1.593 billion, representing 37–38% growth.
