# MISSION SUCCESS: Rocket Lab Launches 97th Electron Mission

- URL: https://rocketlabcorp.com/updates/mission-success-rocket-lab-launches-97th-electron-mission/
- Publisher: Rocket Lab
- Published: 2026-09-26
- Role: 同期履约与累计数

On September 26 Electron placed the 13th StriX satellite in a 559 km orbit for Synspective, its 18th flight of 2026. Rocket Lab said all 13 Synspective deployments had succeeded. The 13 completed missions belong to the 27 previously contracted, rather than being additional to that total.
