# MISSION SUCCESS: Rocket Lab Launches 96th Electron Mission

- URL: https://rocketlabcorp.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/mission-success-rocket-lab-launches-96th-electron-mission
- Publisher: Rocket Lab
- Published: 2026-09-19
- Role: 临近发射及剩余合同

The 12th StriX mission launched September 19 to a 572 km orbit. Rocket Lab reported 17 total launches that year and 15 further dedicated Synspective missions in its manifest through the decade at that point. The next September 26 mission subsequently reduced the remaining preexisting sequence.
