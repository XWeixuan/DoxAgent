# Rocket Lab’s Biggest Launch Deal Yet

- URL: https://rocketlabcorp.com/updates/rocket-labs-biggest-launch-deal-yet-confidential-customer-books-multiple-neutron-and-electron-launches/
- Publisher: Rocket Lab
- Published: 2026-05-07
- Role: 先前合同与积压口径

A confidential customer booked five Neutron and three Electron launches baselined for 2026–2029; management said pricing aligned with average selling prices and did not disclose other terms. The company said its launch manifest exceeded 70 and total backlog exceeded $2.2 billion. It separately described a $190 million HASTE contract for 20 hypersonic tests; HASTE is not Electron orbital service.
