# Rocket Lab Secures Multi-Launch Deal with iQPS

- URL: https://rocketlabcorp.com/updates/rocket-lab-secures-multi-launch-deal-with-iqps-for-three-dedicated-missions/
- Publisher: Rocket Lab
- Published: 2026-07-30
- Role: 其他客户与发射时段竞争

Rocket Lab announced three more dedicated Electron launches for iQPS from late 2027, bringing that customer’s booked total to 18. Each is to deliver a single QPS-SAR satellite; Rocket Lab’s Motorized Lightband separation systems were also purchased for those missions. These bookings represent a different SAR customer.
