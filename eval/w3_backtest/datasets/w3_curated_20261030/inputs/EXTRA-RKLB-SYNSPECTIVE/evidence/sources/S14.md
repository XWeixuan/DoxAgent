# Rocket Lab Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, contract and development context

- URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819994/000181999426000062/rklb-20260630.htm
- Publisher: Rocket Lab / SEC
- Published: 2026-08-10
- Role: 跨业务规模和其他合同

Rocket Lab disclosed a July Space Force agreement for 12 suborbital launches, with options for six more and total potential value $266 million; it separately disclosed a $397 million potential Space-Based Airborne Moving Target Indicator agreement including options. Neutron remained in development and its first-flight schedule depended on qualification and other tests. These are different programs and vehicles.
