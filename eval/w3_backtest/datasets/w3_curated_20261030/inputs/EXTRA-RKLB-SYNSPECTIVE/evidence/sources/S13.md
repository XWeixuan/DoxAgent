# Rocket Lab fiscal Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, launch economics

For the first six months of 2026, Rocket Lab disclosed average revenue per launch of $9.2 million versus $7.5 million in the prior year period. It defines this as average transaction price for launch contract performance obligations in the launch period, regardless of whether recognized at a point in time or over time. It is a realized mixed-contract measure, not the price of the Synspective agreement.
