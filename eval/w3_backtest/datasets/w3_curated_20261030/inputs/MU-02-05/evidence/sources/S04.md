# Micron fiscal Q3 earnings call

Publisher: Micron via Stock Analysis
URL: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mu/transcripts/695617-q3-2026-post-call/
Published: 2026-06-24
Role: company supply and agreement terms

Micron executives told analysts that HBM customer demand in 2027 and 2028 exceeded its ability to supply, and that strategic customer agreements included volumes lower than customers requested. The CFO described customer deposits as cash deposits rather than prepaid revenue, with return over an agreed schedule weighted to the second half of agreement terms. Executives cited approximately $5 billion quarterly enterprise SSD revenue and around $27 billion FY2026 capex; these are company statements and forward plans.
