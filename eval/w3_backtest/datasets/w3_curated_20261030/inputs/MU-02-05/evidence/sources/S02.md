# JPMorgan reiterates rating before results

Publisher: StreetInsider
URL: https://www.streetinsider.com/dr/news.php?id=27108994
Published: 2026-09-28
Role: prior analyst report

On September 28 StreetInsider reported Harlan Sur’s reiteration and reproduced his expectation of results exceeding $51.4 billion, 86.2% gross margin and $31.73 EPS. Sur described moderation in the rate of price increases as a strategic customer relationship and agreement decision. He estimated forward bit production coverage above 35%, with potential for more than 50%, compared with approximately 20% DRAM and 33% NAND volumes previously disclosed by Micron. This is an earlier report of the same analyst’s views.
