# TrendForce June HBM supply analysis

Publisher: TrendForce
URL: https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20260602-13074.html
Published: 2026-06-02
Role: prior supply and economics

TrendForce said annual HBM pricing meant 2026 contract prices had not fully tracked quarterly conventional DRAM price increases. Its analysis estimated DDR5 64GB RDIMM per-wafer revenue surpassed HBM in Q1 2026, influencing supplier allocation. It projected HBM would use 22% of major suppliers’ DRAM wafer input by end 2026 and 30% by end 2027 while providing 9% and 13% of total DRAM bit supply respectively. Those figures are estimates, not company shipment data.
