# JPMorgan expectations ahead of earnings

Publisher: Investing.com
URL: https://www.investing.com/news/analyst-ratings/jpmorgan-reiterates-micron-stock-rating-ahead-of-earnings-93CH-4919491
Published: 2026-09-28
Role: prior analyst account

The September 28 report describes JPMorgan’s consensus reference of $51.4 billion revenue, 86.2% gross margin and $31.73 EPS, and a possible beat and raise. JPMorgan estimated forward bit production covered by agreements around 35%, compared with Micron’s prior disclosure of approximately 20% DRAM and 33% NAND volumes. These are analyst projections and the volume definitions are not the same.
