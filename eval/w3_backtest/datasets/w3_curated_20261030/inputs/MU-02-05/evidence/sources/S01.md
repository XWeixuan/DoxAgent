# JPMorgan says Micron rally may not be over

Publisher: GuruFocus
URL: https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9101998/jpmorgan-says-microns-rally-may-not-be-over
Published: 2026-09-29
Role: event analyst report

GuruFocus attributes to JPMorgan analyst Harlan Sur an expectation that Micron would exceed consensus of $51.4 billion revenue, 86.2% gross margin and $31.73 EPS. Sur interpreted moderated price increases as preserving relationships and long-term SCAs. He estimated SCAs covered over 35% of forward bit production, with contracted volumes potentially over 50%, and expected HBM supply tightness through 2028. GuruFocus also reports that after the second anniversary of definitive CHIPS Act agreements in December management plans higher shareholder returns and, over time, return 100% of excess cash primarily through buybacks. These are reported plans and analyst estimates rather than results.
