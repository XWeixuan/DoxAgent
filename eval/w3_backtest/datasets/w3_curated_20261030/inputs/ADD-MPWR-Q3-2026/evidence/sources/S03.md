# USITC Institutes Section 337 Investigation of Certain Vertical Power Delivery Systems

Publisher: US International Trade Commission

URL: https://www.usitc.gov/press_room/news_release/2026/er1009_69342.htm

Published: 2026-10-09

## Excerpt

The U.S. International Trade Commission (USITC) voted to institute an investigation of certain vertical power delivery systems, components thereof, and computing systems containing the same. The investigation is based on a complaint filed on behalf of Vicor Corporation of Andover, Massachusetts, on September 9, 2026. The complaint, as supplemented, alleges violations of section 337 of the Tariff Act of 1930 in the importation into the United States and sale of certain vertical power delivery systems, components thereof, and computing systems containing the same that infringe certain claims of the patent asserted by the complainant. The complainant requests that the USITC issue a limited exclusion order and cease and desist orders.

The USITC has identified the following respondents in this investigation: Monolithic Power Systems, Inc., Kirkland, Washington; Chengdu Monolithic Power Systems Co., Ltd.; MPS International (Shanghai) Ltd.; and other respondents. By instituting this investigation, the USITC has not yet made any decision on the merits of the case.
