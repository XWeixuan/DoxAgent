# Monolithic Power Systems Reports Third Quarter Results on October 29, 2026

## Excerpt

MPS reported its results after market close on October 29, 2026 and will host a question-and-answer webinar at 2:00 p.m. PT / 5:00 p.m. ET.

Q3 2026 financial summary (Q3 2026 / Q2 2026 / Q3 2025): Revenue $1,202.4M / $980.6M / $737.2M; GAAP gross margin 55.4% / 55.2% / 55.1%; GAAP operating margin 29.7% / 31.0% / 26.5%; GAAP diluted EPS $5.51 / $5.22 / $3.74. Non-GAAP gross margin 55.7% / 55.6% / 55.5%; non-GAAP operating margin 38.6% / 37.5% / 35.4%; non-GAAP diluted EPS $8.19 / $6.50 / $4.73.

Revenue by end market, Q3 2026 / Q2 2026 ($M): Enterprise Data 506.7 / 380.6; Storage & Computing 192.2 / 199.8; Communications 187.4 / 131.5; Automotive 180.7 / 157.1; Industrial 71.4 / 54.8; Consumer 64.0 / 56.8.

Third quarter Enterprise Data revenue of $506.7 million increased 33.2% from the second quarter. The sequential increase was driven by higher sales of our power management solutions for AI and server applications. Third quarter Enterprise Data revenue was up 164.6% year over year. Enterprise Data revenue represented 42.1% of our total third quarter revenue compared with 38.8% in the second quarter of 2026.

For the fourth quarter of 2026 ending December 31, we are forecasting revenue in the range of $1,315 million to $1,385 million; GAAP gross margin 55.2% to 55.8%; non-GAAP gross margin 55.5% to 56.1%.

The company reported annual revenue support capacity above $6 billion and expected $8 billion by the second half of 2027. It expected strong demand across existing enterprise data customers to continue into 2027, and described first orders for 48V automotive power solutions and a large data-converter design win expected to ramp in 2027.

For Q4, the company forecast GAAP operating expenses of $334.0–$342.0 million and non-GAAP operating expenses of $216.8–$220.8 million, with a 15% non-GAAP tax rate for 2026.
