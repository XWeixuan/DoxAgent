# OpenAI annual recurring revenue nears $70 billion

Publisher: Axios
URL: https://www.axios.com/2026/09/29/scoop-openais-annual-recurring-revenue-nears-70b
Published: 2026-09-29T14:15:40Z
Role: original reporting

Sources familiar with OpenAI financials told Axios that annual recurring revenue neared $70 billion, with its annualized run rate up over 70% since the beginning of Q3 and business-to-business revenue up more than 100% over the same period. Axios said OpenAI added more consumer revenue during Q3 than it added in all of 2025; it did not provide costs. An annualized run rate is not realized annual revenue.
