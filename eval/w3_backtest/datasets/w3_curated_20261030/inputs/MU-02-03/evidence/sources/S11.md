# Beyond the memory cycle

Publisher: CSIS
URL: https://www.csis.org/analysis/beyond-memory-cycle-ai-hbm-and-new-semiconductor-shortage
Published: 2026-09-17
Role: industry capacity and cycle

CSIS describes the three leading suppliers prioritizing HBM and server memory, with manufacturing expansion requiring years and advanced packaging and qualification. It notes capacity investments may ease shortage but can create excess supply if AI demand growth slows. Its examples include Micron first Boise wafer output in 2027 and other firms expanding in Korea; cited figures are estimates and plans.
