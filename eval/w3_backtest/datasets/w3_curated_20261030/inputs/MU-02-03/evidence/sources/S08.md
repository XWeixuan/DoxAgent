# Micron fiscal Q3 prepared remarks

Publisher: Micron
URL: https://micron.gcs-web.com/static-files/631b1a32-5537-46ae-8f40-82e42fc79dfe
Published: 2026-06-24
Role: company contract baseline

Micron said 16 signed strategic customer agreements represented approximately 20% of its DRAM volume and one-third of NAND volume over their terms, generally 2026–2030. Fourteen had approximately $100 billion of cumulative contracted revenue at minimum price and volume. Most large agreements have floor and ceiling prices; when all targeted SCAs were completed, Micron expected half or more of revenue to be covered, with fixed or capped pricing on about 40% of revenue. It projected $22 billion in customer financial commitments, about $18 billion in cash deposits, returned over time, generally in the latter half of agreements. Those expected totals and coverage are not actual later signed amounts.
