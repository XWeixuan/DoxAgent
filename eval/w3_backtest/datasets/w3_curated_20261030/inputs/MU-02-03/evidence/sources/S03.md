# OpenAI and NVIDIA system partnership

Publisher: OpenAI
URL: https://openai.com/index/openai-nvidia-systems-partnership/
Published: 2025-09-22
Role: compute transmission

OpenAI and NVIDIA announced a letter of intent to deploy at least 10 GW of NVIDIA systems for next-generation training and inference infrastructure. NVIDIA intended to invest up to $100 billion as systems are deployed; the first GW was targeted for the second half of 2026 using Vera Rubin. Neither the letter nor stated GW is a Micron order.
