# TrendForce third-quarter memory forecast

Publisher: TrendForce
URL: https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20260703-13134.html
Published: 2026-07-03
Role: prior price forecast

TrendForce forecast conventional DRAM contract prices to rise 13–18% quarter on quarter in Q3 2026 and NAND 10–15%. It cited severe DRAM tightness, AI and server demand, weakening consumer affordability, and high comparison bases. Some server purchases were under long-term agreements, while high PC OEM inventory reduced willingness to accept higher client SSD prices.
