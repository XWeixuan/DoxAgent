# NVIDIA Rubin Ultra HBM configuration review

Publisher: TrendForce
URL: https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20260804-13166.html
Published: 2026-08-04
Role: prior specification

TrendForce reported NVIDIA had begun evaluating alternatives to its original 12-Hi HBM4E design, including 8-Hi HBM4E and 8-Hi or 12-Hi HBM4, because of DRAM availability and uncertainty in validation. It said the final specification had not been determined and that some cloud providers were evaluating lower HBM capacities for next-generation ASICs.
