# Memory prices and cloud capex outlook

Publisher: TrendForce
URL: https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20260825-13198.html
Published: 2026-08-25
Role: prior forecast and contract limits

TrendForce projected server DRAM prices up approximately 270% cumulatively in 2026 and enterprise SSD prices up 235%. It said some long-term agreements signed from Q2 2026 included price ceilings, while forecasting 2027 HBM contract prices could rise 70–140%. It projected DRAM plus NAND at 47% of CSP capex in 2026 and 68% in 2027. Contract price ranges and blended ASP have different units.
