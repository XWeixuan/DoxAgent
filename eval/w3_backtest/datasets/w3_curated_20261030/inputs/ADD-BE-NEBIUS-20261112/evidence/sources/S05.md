# Bloom third quarter 2026 results

Publisher: Bloom Energy

## Relevant excerpt

* Achieved record quarterly revenue of $1,235.8 million, delivering 138.1% year-over-year growth
  * Raises full year 2026 revenue guidance to $4.4 billion – $4.6 billion, representing 122% year-over-year growth at the midpoint
  * Added to the S&P 500, marking an important milestone for Bloom Energy as a public company
SAN JOSE, Calif., October 22, 2026—Bloom Energy Corporation (NYSE: BE) (“Bloom,” “Bloom Energy,” “We,” or the “Company”) today reported its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

All comparisons are to the third quarter of 2025.
  * Revenue of $1,235.8 million increased 138.1%. Product revenue of $1,073.6 million increased 179.4%.
  * Gross margin of 33.8% increased 453 basis points. Non-GAAP gross margin of 34.5% increased 412 basis points.
  * Operating income of $225.4 million increased $217.6 million. Non-GAAP operating income of $285.7 million increased $239.4 million.
  * EPS of $0.78 increased $0.88. Non-GAAP EPS of $0.94 increased $0.79.
KR Sridhar, Founder, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Bloom Energy, said, “Electricity is the foundation of economic growth, human well-being and national competitiveness. Today, the world is moving from a century-old model of centralized power to a new architecture that includes firm, distributed onsite generation. Bloom is leading that transformation. What began with hyperscalers is rapidly moving across market segments because every business needs reliable power to grow.
We are building the electricity platform for the digital era: power where it is needed, when it is needed, clean and always on.”
Simon Edwards, Chief Financial Officer of Bloom Energy, added, “Our third quarter results reflect strong execution across the business, with significant revenue growth and expanded operating margins. We continue to invest with discipline to build the capabilities and capacity needed to scale the business. Our year-to-date performance and customer demand give us confidence to raise our 2026 outlook for the third time this year.”
