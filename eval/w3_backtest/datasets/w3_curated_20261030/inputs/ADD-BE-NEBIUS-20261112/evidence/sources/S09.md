# Sustain SJ sues Vineland over DataOne approval, challenging stormwater plans and planning board authority

Jen Russell Data Centers DataOne, Vineland, Vineland Planning Board

South Jersey advocacy organization Sustain SJ filed a lawsuit Tuesday seeking to overturn the City of Vineland Planning Board’s approval of an amended site plan for the DataOne AI data center on South Lincoln Avenue.
The 14-page complaint, filed in Cumberland County Superior Court, alleges that the planning board improperly approved the development despite deficiencies in its stormwater management plans. But the lawsuit goes further, challenging the validity of a 2022 zoning amendment and arguing that the application should have been heard by the city’s zoning board of adjustment.

Sustain SJ names the City of Vineland, its planning board and DataOne USA LLC as defendants.
The lawsuit is an action in lieu of prerogative writs, a legal procedure used to challenge decisions by government bodies, including municipal planning and zoning boards.
## What was approved?

The lawsuit concerns the planning board’s approval of an amended preliminary and final major site plan for the approximately 106.6-acre DataOne property at 3963 South Lincoln Avenue, Block 7503, Lot 35.01.

The planning board held special hearings on August 5 and August 17, 2026. Its decision was memorialized in Resolution No. 6767, dated September 9.

The amended project includes a 129,622-square-foot data center building and a proposed 587,980-square-foot, two-story expansion.
It also includes Bloom Energy fuel cells, cooling equipment, a water treatment plant, a proposed 1.5-million-gallon liquefied natural gas storage tank and a 27-million-gallon water reservoir.
