## Owned AI factories give us greater control over delivery...

Our owned sites will account for the majority of the capacity we bring online from 2027 onwards.

- We have secured all the power and components we plan to bring online in 2027, and are aligning construction with each site’s scheduled power connection.

## Financial update

**Group revenue in Q3 of $980.8 million was up 571% year-over-year, and up 68% compared to Q2.**

Revenue growth in the quarter was driven by new capacity coming online and revenue from our asset-light model.
- In Q3, we recognized approximately $325 million of non-recurring revenue as part of our asset-light model. This reflects an initial phase of a roll-out, as we engage partners to deploy Nebius architecture and the Nebius AI Cloud platform on their funded capacity.
- We are building a strong pipeline of partnership opportunities. As our asset-light footprint grows, we expect revenue from these activities to become a more regular part of our business.
- Under these partnerships, we earn revenue both when we enable our architecture and platform on a partner’s capacity, and as that capacity comes online.
- Nebius AI cloud revenue was $973.8 million in Q3’26, a 643% increase year-over-year from Q3’25. Our Nebius AI cloud business accounted for approximately 99% of total group revenue during the quarter.
- Annualized run-rate revenue (ARR) of $6.4 billion as of the end of September was up 1,062% year-over-year and up 113% from the $3.0 billion reported at the end of June.

## 2026 Guidance

We are updating our 2026 guidance for one change: some third-party capacity we expected to come online this year is now scheduled for the first half of 2027. The contracts behind that capacity remain in place.

- **Revenue:** We now expect revenue at the low end of our $3.0-3.4 billion range.

- **Adjusted EBITDA margin:** Unchanged, at approximately 40%.
- **Capital expenditures:** Approximately $27 billion, above our $20-25 billion range, as we invest in capacity for 2027, in particular at our owned sites.

- **ARR:** We had guided to $7-9 billion by December. We now expect ARR to exceed $7 billion for the month of February, a shift of about two months, even before New Jersey ramps up to full capacity.

- **Contracted power:** We continue to expect 5 GW of contracted power by year-end, from more than 4 GW today.
## 2027: initial outlook

- **Revenue:** $12-13 billion, approximately four times our 2026 revenue.

- **Pre-tax profit:** breakeven in Q4 2027. This is calculated after financing costs, and assumes a five-year depreciation life for compute.

- **Capital expenditures:** $40-$45 billion, sized for more than 1 GW of new capacity. We expect to cover 75-85% of our capex through advanced prepayments and asset-backed financing investment-grade customers along with our balance sheet.
- **Power:** we continue to expect to bring online more than 1 GW of new capacity each year.

- **ARR:** From 2027, we will provide guidance for revenue, pre-tax earnings and capacity. ARR annualizes a single month, and does not reflect the scale of our business.
