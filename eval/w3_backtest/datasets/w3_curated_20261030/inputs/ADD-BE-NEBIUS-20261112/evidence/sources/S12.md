## Relevant excerpt

AMSTERDAM--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Nov 12, 2026--

Nebius Group N.V. (NASDAQ: NBIS), the AI cloud company, today announced its unaudited financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

The Company today also published founder and CEO Arkady Volozh’s quarterly letter to shareholders, available on its investor relations website.

Q3 2026 Financial Highlights: Revenue $980.8 million; adjusted EBITDA $465.8 million; net loss from continuing operations $37.5 million.
