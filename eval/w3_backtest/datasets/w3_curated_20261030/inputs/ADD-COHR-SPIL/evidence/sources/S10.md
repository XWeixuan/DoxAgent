# Coherent fiscal 2026 investor presentation

- URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/820318/000119312526346860/d128030dex992.htm
- Publisher: Coherent / SEC
- Published: 2026-08-12
- Role: 扩产与产品技术

Coherent said its six-inch InP platform produces EMLs, continuous-wave lasers and photodiodes and that it was on track to double internal InP output by year-end and more than double again by 2027. Its presentation described multiple CPO architectures and end-to-end capacity expansion; these are company plans and do not specify SPIL order allocation.
