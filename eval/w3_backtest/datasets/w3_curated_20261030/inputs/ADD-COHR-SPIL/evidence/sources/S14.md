# Coherent fiscal 2026 results segment data

- URL: https://ir.coherent.com/news-releases/news-release-details/coherent-corp-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-fiscal-2026
- Publisher: Coherent
- Published: 2026-08-12
- Role: 目标公司业务基数

For fiscal Q4 Coherent reported Datacenter & Communications revenue of $1.615 billion, up from $1.018 billion a year earlier, and Industrial revenue $430.5 million, compared with $511.1 million. For fiscal 2026 those segments were $5.275 billion and $1.844 billion, respectively. The segments encompass multiple products beyond CPO.
