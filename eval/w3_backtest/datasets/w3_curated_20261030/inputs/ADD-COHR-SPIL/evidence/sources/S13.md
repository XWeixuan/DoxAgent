# Lumentum Announces Fourth Quarter and Full Fiscal Year 2026 Results

- URL: https://investor.lumentum.com/financial-news-releases/news-details/2026/Lumentum-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Fiscal-Year-2026-Results/default.aspx
- Publisher: Lumentum
- Published: 2026-08-11
- Role: 同业规模与光学需求

Lumentum reported fiscal Q4 revenue $1.01 billion and forecast fiscal Q1 $1.225–1.275 billion. It identified optical circuit switching and cloud modules, including 1.6T adoption, as emerging drivers; it also described supply, capacity and possible customer order changes as risks.
