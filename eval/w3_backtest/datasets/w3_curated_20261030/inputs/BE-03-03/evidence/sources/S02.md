# Anthropic IPO prospectus shows surging costs

Publisher: Reuters via StreetInsider
URL: https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Anthropics%2BIPO%2Bprospectus%2Bshows%2Bsweeping%2BAI%2Bvision%2C%2Bsurging%2Bcosts/27115819.html
Published: 2026-09-28T19:17:00-04:00
Role: prior disclosure

Reuters reported the confidential prospectus: 2025 revenue nearly $4.6 billion, operating loss over $8 billion and net loss near $42 billion, including roughly $34 billion of non-cash valuation charges. Compute and infrastructure expense was $7.33 billion; cash and short-term investments were $20.28 billion at year end. Two customers supplied nearly a quarter of revenue, and major clients were not all locked into long-term contracts. Anthropic declined comment.
