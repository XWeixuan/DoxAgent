# Broadcom 8-K TPU agreements

Publisher: Broadcom
URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1730168/000119312526144028/d87999d8k.htm
Published: 2026-04-06
Role: prior partnership terms

Broadcom disclosed that Anthropic would access approximately 3.5 GW of TPU-based compute through Broadcom beginning in 2027. Consumption depends on Anthropic continued commercial success. Separately Broadcom and Google entered long-term TPU and networking supply agreements through up to 2031.
