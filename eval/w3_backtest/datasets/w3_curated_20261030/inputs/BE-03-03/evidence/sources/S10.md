Publisher: Amazon
URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000026/amzn-20260630.htm

Role: prior financing terms

Amazon disclosed an expansion of Anthropic commercial commitments by more than $100 billion over ten years including obligations relating to chip performance.
