# Anthropic to seek $2 trillion IPO valuation

Publisher: Kiplinger
URL: https://www.kiplinger.com/investing/ipos/anthropic-to-seek-trillion-ipo-valuation
Published: 2026-09-29T12:14:18-04:00
Role: contemporaneous context

The prospectus described a possible valuation over $2 trillion, $518 billion in future infrastructure spending and a 12-fold increase in 2025 revenue to nearly $4.6 billion. The reported $42 billion net loss includes approximately $34 billion in accounting charges tied to convertible financing.
