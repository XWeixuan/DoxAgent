# Anthropic $518 billion buildout deals

Publisher: Reuters via MarketScreener
URL: https://www.marketscreener.com/news/anthropic-s-518-billion-ai-buildout-hinges-largely-on-deals-that-cannot-be-canceled-filing-shows-ce785adddd8bf124
Published: 2026-09-29T06:06:00-04:00
Role: primary reporting

Reuters reports a confidential prospectus it reviewed: at least $518 billion over a decade with six partners, approximately 80% noncancelable or payable regardless of use. The reported minimums are $111.1 billion Google, $110 billion Amazon, $31.4 billion Microsoft, and $161.2 billion in largely noncancelable Broadcom equipment leases. Google and Amazon shortfalls must be paid; Microsoft has an uncured material breach exception, while Broadcom leases have default exceptions. Agreements with xAI could require up to $84.5 billion through 2029 and are largely cancelable on 90 days notice. Anthropic describes compute as a constraint and cloud suppliers as investors, customers, distributors and competitors.
