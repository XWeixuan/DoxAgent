# Microsoft NVIDIA and Anthropic partnerships

Publisher: Anthropic
URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/microsoft-nvidia-anthropic-announce-strategic-partnerships
Published: 2025-11-18
Role: prior partnership

Anthropic committed to purchase $30 billion of Azure compute and contract additional capacity up to one gigawatt. Microsoft and Nvidia committed to invest up to $5 billion and $10 billion respectively in Anthropic.
