# Anthropic expands Google Cloud and TPU use

Publisher: Google Cloud
URL: https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2026-04-06-Anthropic-Expands-Use-of-Google-Cloud-and-TPUs
Published: 2026-04-06
Role: prior partnership

Anthropic announced multiple gigawatts of TPU capacity expected to come online beginning in 2027, delivered through Google Cloud and access to Google-built TPUs supplied through Broadcom.
