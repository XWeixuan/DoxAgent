# Anthropic and Amazon expand collaboration

Publisher: Anthropic
URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-amazon-compute
Published: 2026-04-20
Role: prior partnership

Anthropic announced a commitment of more than $100 billion over ten years to AWS technologies and up to 5 GW of capacity, including almost 1 GW expected to come online by the end of 2026. AWS remained its primary training and cloud provider. The agreement spans Graviton and Trainium chips.
