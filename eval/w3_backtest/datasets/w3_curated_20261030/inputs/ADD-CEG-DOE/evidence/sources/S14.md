# Constellation and Meta Clinton agreement

- URL: https://investors.constellationenergy.com/news-releases/news-release-details/constellation-meta-sign-20-year-deal-clean-reliable-nuclear
- Publisher: Constellation / Meta
- Published: 2025-06-03
- Role: 既有机组及增量

Meta signed a 20-year agreement for the 1,121 MW Clinton plant beginning in June 2027 after the state zero-emission credit program expires; it includes a 30 MW planned uprate and supports continued operation and relicensing. The entire plant output is not newly created capacity.
