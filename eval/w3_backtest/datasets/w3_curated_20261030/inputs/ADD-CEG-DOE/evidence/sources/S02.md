# Google and Constellation Announce Landmark Agreement to Bring 890 MW of New Nuclear Capacity to PJM Grid

- URL: https://www.constellationenergy.com/news/2026/10/google-and-constellation-announce-landmark-agreement-to-bring-890-mw-of-new-nuclear-capacity-to-pjm-grid.html
- Publisher: Constellation
- Published: 2026-10-06
- Role: prior customer and project

A 20-year Google agreement supported plans for over $4.3 billion in investment and about 890 MW in nuclear uprates across 11 reactors. It also included approximately 2,700 MW of existing capacity under a 15-year arrangement. The first uprates were expected in 2028.
