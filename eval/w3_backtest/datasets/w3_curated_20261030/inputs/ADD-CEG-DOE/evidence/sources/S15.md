# Constellation second-quarter 2026 financial results

- Publisher: Constellation
- Published: 2026-08-06
- Role: 公司盈利及指引

Constellation reported second-quarter GAAP net income of $1.42 per share and adjusted operating earnings of $2.55 per share. It raised full-year adjusted operating earnings guidance to $11.50–$12.50 per share. Management cited Calpine integration and corporate power agreements as part of its broader operating context.
