# Energy Department Announces $5 Billion Loan to Boost Nuclear Power and Help Lower Costs in Illinois, Maryland, and Pennsylvania

- URL: https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-department-announces-5-billion-loan-boost-nuclear-power-and-help-lower-costs
- Publisher: US Department of Energy
- Published: 2026-10-27
- Role: primary announcement

DOE Office of Energy Dominance Financing announced a conditional commitment of up to $5 billion to finance upgrades and modernization across Constellation nuclear facilities in Illinois, Maryland and Pennsylvania. DOE says the projects would add nearly 1.1 GW of electricity serving the PJM and MISO regions. The planned sites are Braidwood, Byron, Clinton, Dresden, LaSalle and Quad Cities in Illinois; Calvert Cliffs in Maryland; and Limerick in Pennsylvania. The conditional commitment also includes an option to finance future uprates at additional Constellation facilities. Before definitive financing documents and funding, Constellation must satisfy technical, legal, environmental and financial conditions. DOE describes the commitment as intent to provide financing, not a completed funded loan.
