# Google and Constellation nuclear uprates technical details

- URL: https://www.powermag.com/google-deal-backs-890-mw-of-constellation-nuclear-uprates-at-11-pjm-reactors/
- Publisher: POWER
- Published: 2026-10-07

The report distinguishes the 20-year contract for 890 MW of planned incremental output from a separate 15-year, 2,700 MW existing-fleet supply agreement. It reports the first uprate expected in 2028 and says the NRC expected-applications list had Constellation planning 12 uprate filings between late 2026 and 2030, including different application types.
