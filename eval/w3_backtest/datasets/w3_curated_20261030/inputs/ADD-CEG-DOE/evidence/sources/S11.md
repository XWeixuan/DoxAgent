# Google on its Constellation uprate agreement

- URL: https://blog.google/company-news/why-were-backing-americas-existing-nuclear-plants/
- Publisher: Google
- Published: 2026-10-06
- Role: 客户回应与承购作用

Google said it would be anchor customer for 890 MW of upgrades at 11 existing Constellation reactors, giving long-term revenue certainty for investment. Google expected all 890 MW by the end of 2032 and described an additional collaboration on Google Cloud. The customer statement predates the DOE financing announcement.
