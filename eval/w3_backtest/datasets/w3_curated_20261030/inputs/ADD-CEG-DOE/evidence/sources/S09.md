# DOE launches UPRISE for existing nuclear fleet

- URL: https://www.energy.gov/ne/articles/nations-nuclear-reactor-fleet-rise
- Publisher: US Department of Energy
- Published: 2026-03-12
- Role: 融资政策先验

DOE described UPRISE as supporting output uprates, restarts and plant efficiency, targeting 2.5 GW additional capacity by 2027 and 5 GW by 2029. It said its financing office could provide up to 80% of eligible uprate project costs. This is a program-level capability and not the terms of a specific loan commitment.
