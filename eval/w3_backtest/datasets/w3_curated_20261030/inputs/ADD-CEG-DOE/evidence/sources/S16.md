# NRC backgrounder on nuclear power uprates

- URL: https://www.nrc.gov/regulations-legislation/fact-sheets-brochures/backgrounder-on-power-uprates-for-nuclear-plants
- Publisher: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
- Published: 2022-01
- Role: 许可和工程约束

The NRC explains that changing licensed reactor power requires an application and safety review. Extended uprates can require significant changes to equipment such as turbines, generators and transformers; applicants must show the modified plant can continue to operate safely. The process is distinct from a customer agreement or financing commitment.
