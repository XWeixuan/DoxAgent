# Constellation and Walmart Dresden PPA

- URL: https://investors.constellationenergy.com/node/10101/pdf
- Publisher: Constellation / Walmart
- Published: 2026-06-23
- Role: 既有非科技承购

A Dresden agreement includes about 176 MW of wholesale supply, including a 30 MW planned uprate. Walmart purchases energy, capacity and environmental attributes under two 15-year terms beginning in 2029 and 2030. Planned incremental output is part of the larger supply figure.
