# Energy Department Announces $4.2 Billion Investment to Boost Nuclear Power in Pennsylvania and Ohio

- URL: https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-department-announces-42-billion-investment-boost-nuclear-power-and-help-lower
- Publisher: US Department of Energy
- Published: 2026-10-05
- Role: prior industry comparison

DOE previously announced up to $4.2 billion in conditional financing for Vistra uprates and modernization at Beaver Valley, Davis-Besse and Perry. DOE said the work would preserve nearly 4 GW, add 433 MW and support operations for an additional 20 years beyond existing licenses; potential future uprates at Comanche Peak were an option. Technical, legal, environmental and financial conditions remained before definitive financing and funding.
