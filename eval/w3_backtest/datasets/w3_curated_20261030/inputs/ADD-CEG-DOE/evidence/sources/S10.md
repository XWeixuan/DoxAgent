# Constellation and Amazon Calvert Cliffs agreement

- URL: https://www.constellationenergy.com/news/2026/09/constellation-and-amazon-announce-20-year-power-purchase-agreement-at-calvert-cliffs.html
- Publisher: Constellation / Amazon
- Published: 2026-09-30
- Role: 已有项目采购和投资

The 20-year agreement covers 690 MW including a planned 190 MW uprate at the 1,790 MW Calvert Cliffs plant. The companies said the arrangement enables more than $3 billion of investment in improvements and output, with added capacity expected between 2030 and 2032. Electricity continues to flow to PJM; the 190 MW is part of, not additional to, 690 MW.
