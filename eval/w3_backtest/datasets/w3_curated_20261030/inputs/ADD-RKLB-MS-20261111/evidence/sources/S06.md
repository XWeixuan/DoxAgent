## Neutron backlog grows to 16 missions, and base launch price rises to $70M, reflecting strong customer confidence ahead of debut as launch capacity tightens across the industry
LONG BEACH, Calif., Nov. 09, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Rocket Lab Corporation (Nasdaq: RKLB), a global leader in launch and space systems, today announced a record quarter of Neutron launch contracts: eight missions across five constellation operators totaling more than $515 million, including several missions sold at an elevated base price of $70 million per launch.
- European synthetic aperture radar constellation manufacturer and operator **ICEYE** has signed a contract for three dedicated Neutron launches beginning from late 2028.

- U.S.-based satellite manufacturer and constellation operator **Muon Space** has signed a contract for two dedicated Neutron launches, one in 2029 and one in 2030.

- U.S.-based company **Starcloud**, which plans to design, build, and deploy data centers in space, has also booked a dedicated Neutron launch in 2029.
- These contracts follow on from a single dedicated Neutron launch booked by Canadian satellite communications provider **Kepler Communications**, which was signed in Q3 and recently announced, and a single dedicated Neutron launch for the United States Space Force under the Space-Based Airborne Moving Target Indicator (SB-AMTI) program signed in Q3.
Huge demand for Neutron ahead of its debut has grown its launch manifest to 16 missions, with the next available launch opportunities now in the 2030s. The base launch price is now $70 million, up from $55 million, reflecting strong customer confidence in and demand for the vehicle amid tightening launch availability across the industry.
