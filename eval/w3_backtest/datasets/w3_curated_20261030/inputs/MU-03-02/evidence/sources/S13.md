# HBM Industry Analysis - 3Q26

- URL: https://www.trendforce.com/research/download/RP260805KC3
- Publisher: TrendForce
- Published: 2026-08-05
- Role: 行业供需及产品组合

TrendForce’s published summary described expanding HBM supply, SK hynix leading and Samsung gaining, while HBM3E remained dominant and HBM4 began ramping. It expected HBM average selling price weakness in 2026 and growth in 2027 as product mix shifted. This is a third-party forecast rather than Micron ASP guidance.
