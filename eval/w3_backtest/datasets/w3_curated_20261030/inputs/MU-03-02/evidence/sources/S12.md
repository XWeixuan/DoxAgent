# Alphabet Second Quarter 2026 Earnings Call

- URL: https://abc.xyz/investor/events/event-details/2026/2026-Q2-Earnings-Call-2026-GgTAq7Is0z/default.aspx
- Publisher: Alphabet
- Published: 2026-07-22
- Role: 终端AI基础设施支出基线

Alphabet reported $44.9 billion of Q2 capex, mostly technical infrastructure for AI; roughly 60% of technical infrastructure investment was servers and 40% data centers and networking. It also reported negative quarterly free cash flow of $5.9 billion and said it was using third-party capacity as a bridge while building internal supply. Alphabet capex is not Micron orders.
