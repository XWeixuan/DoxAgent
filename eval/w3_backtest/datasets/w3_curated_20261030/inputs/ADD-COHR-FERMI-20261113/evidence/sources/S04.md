Termination of TensorWave Lease

Hyperscaler Agreements
On November 10, 2026, the Company entered into a non-binding letter of intent with an investment-grade hyperscaler for the delivery of approximately 600 MW of powered land at Project Matador. In connection with the letter of intent, the Company and the hyperscaler have entered into a $200,000 prepayment agreement and a $15,000 early construction and site development agreement.
These agreements had no effect on the unaudited condensed consolidated financial statements as of and for the periods ended September 30, 2026.
Amendment to Turbine Warehouse Equipment Financing
On October 9, 2026, Fermi Turbine Warehouse LLC and Firebird Equipment Holdco, LLC, as subsidiary guarantor, entered into a Limited Waiver and Amendment No. 1 to the Turbine Warehouse Equipment Financing (“Amendment No. 1”) with MUFG, as administrative agent, and the lenders party thereto. Among other things, Amendment No.
1 set the minimum quarterly principal payment at 5% of the aggregate principal amount of loans outstanding and eliminated the condition under which that payment would have increased, and the administrative agent could have begun marketing the financed equipment, if the Company had not entered into a lease or offtake arrangement for at least 400 MW at Project Matador by November 10, 2026.
It did not modify the separate requirement that the Company consummate project financing for the first phase of Project Matador by February 10, 2027, described in Note 5, Debt, net, which remains in effect.
