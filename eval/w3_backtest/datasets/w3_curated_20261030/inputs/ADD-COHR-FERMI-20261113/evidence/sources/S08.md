## Relevant excerpt

## STRONG DEMAND AND MANUFACTURING EXECUTION

- Datacenter and Communications revenue grew 79% YoY, representing 82% of total revenue
- New LTAs strengthen multiyear visibility, including a PhotonLink™ agreement valued at ~$20B over five years; with revenue beginning to ramp in early CY2028
- Internal InP output capacity doubled YoY one quarter early; on track to more than double again by CY27-end
## SCALE-ACROSS AND PHOTONLINK EXPAND GROWTH

- Scale-across / DCI: Q1 revenue exceeded $200M, roughly quadrupling YoY and reaching a nearly $1B annualized revenue run rate; quarterly revenue expected to roughly double from Q1 by CY2027-end
- PhotonLink: Revenue expected to exceed $100M in the March 2027 quarter and $250M per quarter before CY27-end
