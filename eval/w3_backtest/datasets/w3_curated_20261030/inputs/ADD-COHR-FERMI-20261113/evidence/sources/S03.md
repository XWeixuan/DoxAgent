# Fermi hyperscaler agreements and termination

## Relevant excerpt

FRMI-7.04%
DALLAS - Fermi Inc. (NASDAQ:FRMI)(LSE:FRMI) announced today it has entered into agreements with an undisclosed investment-grade hyperscale customer for 600 megawatts of power capacity at Project Matador, beginning in 2027, according to a press release statement.
The agreements include a $200 million prepayment from the customer and a $15 million early construction and site development agreement. The parties are finalizing an additional $90 million structural steel and building materials purchase agreement. The customer’s identity remains confidential, and the parties continue to work toward a definitive long-term lease.
Fermi also disclosed the termination of its Data Center Lease and Services Agreement with TensorWave, which was signed in August 2026. The associated AMD credit support agreement was also terminated.
For the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, Fermi reported a net loss of $141.6 million, or $0.22 per share. General and administrative expenses totaled $142.2 million, including $65.8 million of non-cash stock compensation and $21.5 million of financial advisory fees settled through common stock issuance. The company remains pre-revenue.
As of September 30, 2026, Fermi held $315.5 million in total cash and restricted cash. The company invested $133.4 million in property, plant, and equipment during the quarter, bringing the net balance to approximately $1.67 billion. Outstanding debt, net, stood at $958.4 million, reflecting a $463.0 million net increase in borrowings.

For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, capital invested in property, plant, and equipment totaled $759.5 million.
Fermi is developing Project Matador in the Texas Panhandle as a private electric grid for AI infrastructure. The company scheduled a conference call for today at 9 a.m. Eastern Time to discuss the quarterly results.
