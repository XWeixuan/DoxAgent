Major Customers

One major customer accounted for 20%, 10%, and 10% of consolidated revenue during fiscal 2026, 2025 and 2024, respectively. A second major customer accounted for 12% of consolidated revenue during fiscal 2026. A third major customer accounted for 12% of consolidated revenue during fiscal 2025. Revenue from these customers was primarily attributable to our Datacenter &amp; Communications segment.
