# DRAM industry Q2 2026 results

Publisher: TrendForce
URL: https://www.trendforce.com.tw/presscenter/news/20260907-13218.html
Published: 2026-09-07
Role: industry supplier comparison

TrendForce estimated industry DRAM revenue grew 59.5% sequentially in Q2 2026 to $154.73 billion, with only modest bit-shipment growth. Samsung revenue share was 39.4%, SK hynix 24.9%, and Micron 23.3%, with Micron DRAM revenue up 65.5% sequentially to $36 billion. It expected the major suppliers to increase bit output mainly through advanced process transitions during 2026–27.
