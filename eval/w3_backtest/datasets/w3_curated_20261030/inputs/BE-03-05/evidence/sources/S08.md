# PJM 2028/2029 capacity auction results

- URL: https://insidelines.pjm.com/pjm-capacity-auction-procures-138318-mw-of-generation-resources-as-work-continues-to-address-growing-electricity-demand/
- Publisher: PJM
- Published: 2026-07-14
- Role: 缺口与容量基线

PJM procured 138,318 MW of unforced capacity in its auction, plus 10,864 MW through fixed resource requirements. Cleared capacity was 6,831 MW below the reliability requirement and the entire region cleared at the $325/MW-day price cap. PJM said the shortfall implies thinner reserves and greater risk, not necessarily an inability to serve load. The auction value is not equivalent to total load cost because of self-supply and bilateral hedges.
