# PJM board directs actions for resource adequacy and large loads

- URL: https://insidelines.pjm.com/pjm-board-directs-action-on-resource-adequacy-affordability-and-large-loads/
- Publisher: PJM
- Published: 2026-07-27
- Role: 原提案机制与资格

PJM proposed a central backstop with commitments up to 15 years and a $555/MW-day aggregate offer cap. New generation and annual demand response and distributed energy resources could qualify subject to specified requirements; resources had to be online no later than June 1, 2032, and new projects would pass standard interconnection studies. Distributed-resource aggregators had to show sites and contracts for the term. Bilateral arrangements were a parallel track.
