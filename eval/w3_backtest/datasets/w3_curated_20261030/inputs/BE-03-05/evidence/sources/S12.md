# Bloom Energy 2025 annual report: AEP and Brookfield

- URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1664703/000162828026006516/be-20251231.htm
- Publisher: Bloom Energy / SEC
- Published: 2026-02
- Role: Bloom渠道与融资

Bloom described an AEP agreement for up to 1 GW of fuel cells with an initial 100 MW order and potential later phases. Separately its Brookfield framework provides up to $5 billion over five years for future eligible projects subject to investment and contractual criteria. Bloom sells to financiers, strategic partners, distributors and end customers; a funding framework is not itself an equipment order.
