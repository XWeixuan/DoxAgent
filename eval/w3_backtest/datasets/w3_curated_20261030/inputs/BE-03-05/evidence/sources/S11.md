# AEP Energy interpretation of PJM capacity auction

- URL: https://www.aepenergy.com/blog/what-the-2028-2029-pjm-capacity-auction-means/
- Publisher: AEP Energy
- Published: 2026-07-15
- Role: 用户电价与反证

AEP Energy noted that the 2028/29 auction cleared at $325/MW-day and estimated the uncapped simulation at approximately $554.72/MW-day, while noting bidder behavior could differ without the cap. The capacity shortfall lowered the reserve margin to 14.7%; PJM stated that the shortfall does not itself establish that reliability problems will occur.
