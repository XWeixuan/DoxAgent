# Bloom and Equinix expand onsite power agreement

- URL: https://investor.bloomenergy.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2025/Bloom-Energy-Expands-Data-Center-Power-Agreement-with-Equinix-Surpassing-100MW/default.aspx
- Publisher: Bloom Energy / Equinix
- Published: 2025-02-20
- Role: 实际现场业务案例

The companies said their relationship exceeded 100 MW of fuel cell capacity, with approximately 75 MW operating and 30 MW under construction. Bloom said fuel cells supplemented grid power at 19 Equinix data centers across six states. Operating and construction capacities are distinct, and the agreement is not a PJM backstop award.
