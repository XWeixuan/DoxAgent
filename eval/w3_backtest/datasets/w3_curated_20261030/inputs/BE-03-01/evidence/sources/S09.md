# Nebius and Bloom partner on AI infrastructure

Publisher: Nebius
URL: https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-and-bloom-energy-partner-to-power-ai-infrastructure-build-out
Published: 2026-05-20
Role: customer deployment

Nebius and Bloom announced behind-the-meter fuel cell deployment. The first project was planned for 328 MW operational during 2026, replacing previously planned reciprocating engines. Nebius cited deployment speed and permitting; the release explicitly treats timing and further deployments as forward-looking.
