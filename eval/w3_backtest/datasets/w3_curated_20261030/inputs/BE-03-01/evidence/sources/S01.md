# Ameren Missouri updates 20-year energy roadmap

Publisher: Ameren Missouri
URL: https://ameren.mediaroom.com/2026-09-28-Ameren-Missouri-updates-20-year-energy-roadmap-to-maintain-and-develop-cost-effective-energy-resources-to-keep-grid-reliable-for-customers
Published: 2026-09-28
Role: primary resource plan

Ameren Missouri describes its preferred plan, selected from over 20 alternatives, as a 20-year roadmap under varying customer demand scenarios. It includes 500 MW of natural gas fuel cells by 2030, 1,900 MW simple-cycle gas by 2029, 2,100 MW combined cycle by 2031, 1,300 MW solar and 2,400 MW batteries by 2030. It reports signed electric service agreements for 2.8 GW of aggregate large-load demand by 2030. A resource plan is a forward-looking plan; this announcement does not name a fuel-cell supplier.
