# Bloom Energy doubles Fremont footprint

Publisher: City of Fremont
URL: https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/CAFREMONT/bulletins/42c66a4
Published: 2026-09-25
Role: primary city announcement

Fremont stated that Bloom had acquired a 158,000-square-foot facility on Encyclopedia Circle, doubling its footprint in the city, and that the project would add hundreds of advanced manufacturing jobs. The city said expansion would eventually support 5 GW of clean power; it did not provide a date or achieved production output for that figure.
