# Bloom and Oracle expand partnership

Publisher: Bloom Energy
URL: https://investor.bloomenergy.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2026/Bloom-Energy-and-Oracle-Expand-Strategic-Partnership-to-Deploy-up-to-2-8-GW-to-Accelerate-AI-Infrastructure-Build-Out/default.aspx
Published: 2026-04-13
Role: business connection

Under a master services agreement, Oracle intends to procure up to 2.8 GW of Bloom fuel cells. An initial 1.2 GW was contracted, with deployment underway and continuing into the following year. The master agreement ceiling and contracted capacity have different statuses.
