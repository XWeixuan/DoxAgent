# Bloom annual report: production capacity

Publisher: Bloom Energy
URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1664703/000162828026006516/be-20251231.htm
Published: 2026-02-09
Role: prior capacity

Bloom stated that it was expanding annual production capacity at Fremont from 1 GW to 2 GW and expected completion by end of 2026. This was an expected manufacturing capacity run rate rather than installed customer power or accepted output.
