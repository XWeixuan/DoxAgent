# Intel Kiryat Gat expansion frozen for foreseeable future

- URL: https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-intel-kiryat-gat-expansion-frozen-for-foreseeable-future-1001515435
- Publisher: Globes
- Published: 2025-07-10
- Role: 工厂项目状态

Globes reported that expansion of the planned Fab 38 had been frozen and the first completed plant still awaited production equipment, while the second part remained on hold. The report separately identified the existing Fab 28 in Kiryat Gat. Planned Fab 38 and existing Fab 28 are distinct facilities.
