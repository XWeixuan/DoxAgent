# Worker dies after explosion at Intel’s Kiryat Gat plant

- URL: https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/914lhis9l
- Publisher: CTech
- Published: 2026-10-14
- Role: 同期设施事故调查

Police described a suspected gas-cylinder malfunction during a material transfer at Intel’s Kiryat Gat site. One worker had died at the time of this report and others were seriously injured. The police commander said investigators had not identified suspected criminal activity or negligence at that stage.
