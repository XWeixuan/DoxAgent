# Kushner urges Netanyahu to test Hamas disarmament in Gaza

- URL: https://www.axios.com/2026/08/17/kushner-netanyahu-hamas-gaza
- Publisher: Axios
- Published: 2026-08-17T18:41:29Z
- Role: 先前各方立场

A US official described a meeting in which Kushner urged small steps to test Hamas disarmament and Netanyahu expressed skepticism about the commitment. The report described differences over strikes against imminent threats and a proposed mechanism for reporting threats, as well as working groups on demilitarization and humanitarian conditions.
