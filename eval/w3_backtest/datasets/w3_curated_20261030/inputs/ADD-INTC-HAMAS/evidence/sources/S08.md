# Intel 2025 Annual Report

- URL: https://www.intc.com/filings-reports/annual-reports/content/0000050863-26-000011/0000050863-26-000011.pdf
- Publisher: Intel
- Published: 2026-01-23
- Role: 生产网络与以色列暴露

Intel identified Israel as a key Intel 7 production fab location in 2025, alongside Arizona, Oregon and Ireland. It said Intel 7 accounted for a majority of internal processor production and products by revenue in 2025 and was expected to account for almost half in 2026. Arizona also produced Intel 7; Israel is one site in the production network.
