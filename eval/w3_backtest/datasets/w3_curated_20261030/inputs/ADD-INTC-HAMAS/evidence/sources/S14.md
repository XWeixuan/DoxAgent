# Hamas leaders tell Kushner they will demilitarize Gaza, sources say

- URL: https://www.axios.com/2026/08/16/kushner-hamas-gaza-disarmament-egypt-israel
- Publisher: Axios
- Published: 2026-08-16T20:13:03Z
- Role: Hamas先前表态及条件

A source with direct knowledge told Axios that Hamas leaders reaffirmed a commitment to demilitarize in a meeting with Kushner. Hamas’s subsequent statement cited a permanent ceasefire, full Israeli withdrawal, aid and reconstruction; Axios noted that statement did not expressly mention disarmament.
