# Amazon Q3 Preview: Analyst Expects AWS Growth To Outperform, Drive Upside

Publisher: Benzinga

URL: https://www.benzinga.com/analyst-stock-ratings/analyst-color/26/10/62596000/amazon-q3-preview-analyst-expects-aws-growth-to-outperform-drive-upside

Published: 2026-10-26T12:09:00-04:00

## Relevant text

On Thursday, Oct. 29, Post expects Amazon to report revenue of $202 billion, versus a consensus of $202.2 billion; EBIT of $26.4 billion, versus consensus of $26.1 billion.

The AWS segment is likely to grow revenue 40% year-on-year, surpassing Street expectations of 38%, he added. While Wall Street projects AWS margins of 34.7%, Post expects higher margins of 39%. Key catalysts include strong backlog growth and expanding adoption of custom silicon and Bedrock, he noted.

While Prime Day’s timing temporarily slowed ad growth to 15%, acceleration is expected in the fourth quarter, he added. Revenue guidance is expected at $239-$246 billion, or $242.5 billion at the midpoint, with operating profit of $29-$34 billion, the analyst stated.
