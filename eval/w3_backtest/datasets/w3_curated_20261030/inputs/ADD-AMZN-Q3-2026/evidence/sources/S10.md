# Coherent Q4 and fiscal 2026 results

Publisher: Coherent / SEC

URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/820318/000119312526346860/d128030dex991.htm

Published: 2026-08-12

## Relevant excerpts

Revenue for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 was $2.05 billion, with GAAP gross margin of 38.5% and GAAP net income of $1.19 per diluted share. On a non-GAAP basis, gross margin was 40.2% with net income per diluted share of $1.74.

Datacenter & Communications revenue for Q4 FY26 was $1,615.0 million, versus $1,361.6 million in Q3 FY26 and $1,018.3 million in Q4 FY25. Industrial revenue was $430.5 million, versus $444.0 million in Q3 FY26 and $511.1 million in Q4 FY25.

“As AI datacenter architectures increasingly transition from copper to optical connectivity, we believe Coherent’s broad photonic technology portfolio and manufacturing scale uniquely position us to deliver accelerating growth and capitalize on this multi-year opportunity,” said CEO Jim Anderson.
