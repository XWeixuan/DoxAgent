# Amazon.com Announces Third Quarter Results

Publisher: Amazon / Business Wire

URL: https://www.streetinsider.com/Business%2BWire/Amazon.com%2BAnnounces%2BThird%2BQuarter%2BResults/27257584.html

Published: 2026-10-29T16:01:00-04:00

## Excerpt

Net sales increased 12% to $201.9 billion in the third quarter, compared with $180.2 billion in third quarter 2025. Excluding the $0.9 billion unfavorable impact from year-over-year changes in foreign exchange rates throughout the quarter, net sales increased 13% compared with third quarter 2025. As a reminder, Prime Day timing shifted this year, with the event occurring in Q2 for most large countries. In 2025, Prime Day occurred entirely in Q3. Excluding this shift, third quarter year-over-year growth would have been approximately 400 basis points higher.

North America segment sales increased 6% year-over-year to $112.9 billion. International segment sales increased 5% year-over-year to $43.1 billion, or increased 8% excluding changes in foreign exchange rates. AWS segment sales increased 39% year-over-year to $46.0 billion.

Operating income increased to $28.1 billion in the third quarter, compared with $17.4 billion in third quarter 2025. Net income decreased to $20.7 billion in the third quarter, or $1.89 per diluted share, compared with $21.2 billion, or $1.95 per diluted share, in third quarter 2025. This decrease is driven primarily by pre-tax gains of $9.5 billion in third quarter 2025 included in non-operating income (expense) from our investments in Anthropic.

Free cash flow decreased to an outflow of $42.4 billion for the trailing twelve months, driven primarily by a year-over-year increase of $94.8 billion in purchases of property and equipment, net of proceeds from sales and incentives. This increase primarily reflects investments in artificial intelligence.

Fourth Quarter 2026 Guidance: Net sales are expected to be between $240.0 billion and $247.0 billion. Advertising revenue grew 12% year-over-year excluding changes in foreign exchange rates in the third quarter 2026 impacted by timing anomalies primarily related to Prime Day (and a couple other timing factors). We expect year-over-year advertising revenue to grow in the high teens in fourth quarter 2026. Operating income is expected to be between $28.5 billion and $33.5 billion. We continue to expect 2026 cash capital expenditures to be approximately $220 billion.
