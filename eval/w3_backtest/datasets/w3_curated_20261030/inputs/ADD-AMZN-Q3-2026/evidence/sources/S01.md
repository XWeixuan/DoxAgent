# Amazon Shares Drop as Huge AI Spending and Ad Revenue Miss Weigh on Q3 Results

## Excerpt

The company's advertising division brought in $19.6B, missing the $20.87B expected, while total net sales reached $201.9B against a $202.3B estimate.

The losses in market value followed a report otherwise marked by efficiency and cloud strength, including a record operating margin of 13.9% and Amazon Web Services revenue growth of 39% to $46B. AWS currently operates at an annualized run rate of $184B, with Chief Executive Officer Andy Jassy forecasting growth rates above 40% for both 2027 and 2028.

To sustain this expansion, the company maintained its cash capital expenditure forecast of approximately $220B for 2026, while Jassy suggested that the company's long-term trajectory could lead to multiple businesses generating $1 trillion in annual revenue within the next decade.
