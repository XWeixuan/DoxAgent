# Amazon Q3 earnings preview – AWS sold out, but how much more will it spend?

Publisher: IG

URL: https://www.ig.com/uk/news-and-trade-ideas/amazon-q3-earnings-preview-aws-sold-out-but-how-much-more-wil-it-spend-261028

Published: 2026-10-28T12:34:51+00:00

## Relevant text

Amazon will report third-quarter results on 29 October, with analysts expecting revenue of around $202.3bn, a 12.3% increase from $180.2bn a year earlier, and earnings per share of $1.96. Revenue estimates range from $199.4bn to $209.6bn and EPS forecasts from $1.72 to $2.32, while consensus EBITDA stands at $53.6bn.

Amazon Web Services remains the main focus, with segment revenue expected at $45.6bn. Third-party seller services are forecast at $45.1bn, advertising at $20.9bn and subscription services at $13.9bn. Chief executive Andy Jassy told Andreessen Horowitz on 23 October that AWS capacity was sold out for 2026 and largely sold out for 2027, with a substantial portion of 2028 also committed.

Investors will look for any update to the company’s capital expenditure guidance of about $220bn for 2026, as well as AWS margins and demand for AI inference through its Bedrock platform.
