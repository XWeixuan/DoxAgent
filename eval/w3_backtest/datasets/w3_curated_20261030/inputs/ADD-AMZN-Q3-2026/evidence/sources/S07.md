# Amazon earnings preview: Q3 2026

Publisher: S&P Global Market Intelligence

URL: https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/research/2026/10/amazon-earnings-preview-q3-2026

Published: 2026-10-28

## Relevant text

According to Visible Alpha consensus, total North American revenue expectations for the third quarter have edged down to $113.8 billion from the prior quarter. Expectations for the international online retail segment have also softened.

In contrast, consensus for Amazon Web Services (AWS) has increased to approximately $45.4 billion. The AWS margin for the third quarter is expected to be 37.3%. This is up from the 35.0% expected last quarter and 34.2% in April 2026. The range of estimates for the third quarter AWS margin has widened 200 bps to 31.4% to 41.1%, with consensus at 37.3%, likely reflecting differing views and assumptions around the profitability of AI services.
