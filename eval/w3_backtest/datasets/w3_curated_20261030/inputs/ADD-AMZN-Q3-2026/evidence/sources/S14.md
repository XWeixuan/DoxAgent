# Amazon announces agreement with Corning to boost US fiber optics manufacturing

Publisher: Amazon

URL: https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-corning-fiber-optics-1000-jobs-north-carolina

Published: 2026-06

## Relevant excerpts

Amazon’s multiyear, multibillion-dollar agreement with Corning will produce optical fiber for data centers and strengthen the U.S. supply chain.

Amazon announced a multibillion-dollar agreement with Corning Incorporated, a leading manufacturer of advanced glass and fiber optic technology, to supply the optical fiber, cable, and connectivity solutions that power Amazon's expanding data center infrastructure across the United States.
