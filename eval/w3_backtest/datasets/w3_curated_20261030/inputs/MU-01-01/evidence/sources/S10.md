# SK hynix Ships Samples of 12-Layer Next-Gen HBM4E

- URL: https://news.skhynix.com/en/sk-hynix-ships-samples-of-12-layer-next-gen-hbm4e-2/
- Publisher: SK hynix
- Published: 2026-06-18
- Role: 另一竞争者产品状态

SK hynix reported shipping 12-layer HBM4E samples to major customers and said it would work with partners on timely mass production. It described 48 GB capacity and maximum 16 Gbps per pin. Sampling is the disclosed stage, not a confirmed mass-production shipment.
