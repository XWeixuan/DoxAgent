# Samsung Ships Industry-First Commercial HBM4

- URL: https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-ships-industry-first-commercial-hbm4-with-ultimate-performance-for-ai-computing
- Publisher: Samsung
- Published: 2026-02-12
- Role: 原有竞品生产与工艺

Samsung announced HBM4 mass production and commercial shipments using 1c DRAM and a 4nm logic base die. It anticipated 2026 HBM sales more than tripling versus 2025 and said it was expanding HBM4 capacity. These are Samsung’s statements from February, prior to the reported September 4nm expansion review.
