# Micron in High-Volume Production of HBM4 Designed for NVIDIA Vera Rubin

- URL: https://stage-investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2026/Micron-in-High-Volume-Production-of-HBM4-Designed-for-NVIDIA-Vera-Rubin-PCIe-Gen6-SSD-and-SOCAMM2-03-16-2026/default.aspx
- Publisher: Micron
- Published: 2026-03-16
- Role: 目标公司产品和平台

Micron announced high-volume production of 36GB 12-high HBM4 designed for NVIDIA Vera Rubin, alongside SOCAMM2 and PCIe Gen6 SSDs. The 48GB 16-high HBM4 was sampling. Company disclosures about its own products complement, rather than replace, third-party supply estimates.
