# Micron and Anthropic Announce Strategic Agreement

- URL: https://micron.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/micron-and-anthropic-announce-strategic-agreement-scale-next
- Publisher: Micron
- Published: 2026-06-22
- Role: 需求传导与多产品合同

Micron and Anthropic announced cooperation on AI memory architecture, a memory and storage supply agreement across the data-center portfolio, Claude adoption at Micron, and a Micron investment in Anthropic. The announcement did not disclose a specific HBM volume or contract price.
