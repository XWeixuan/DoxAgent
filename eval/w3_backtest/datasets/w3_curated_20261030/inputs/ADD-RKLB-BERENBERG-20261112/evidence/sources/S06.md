# Morgan Stanley and peers before Q3

Published: 2026-11-06

## Relevant excerpt

Space systems and launch services provider Rocket Lab (RKLB) will announce its third-quarter earnings on Monday, November 9. Shares have been volatile recently as investors are concerned about AI trades and high-valuation growth stocks. Ahead of Q3 2026 earnings, most analysts are bullish on RKLB stock. Wall Street expects RKLB to report a wider loss per share of $0.07 for Q3 2026 compared to a loss per share of $0.03 in the prior-year quarter.
Revenue is projected to rise about 66% year-over-year to $257.21 million.
## Analysts’ Views Ahead of Q3 Earnings

Rocket Lab recently announced a delay in the launch of the Neutron rocket from Q4 2026 to 2027 after it found defects in a first-stage tank component. Following the news, Citizens analyst Trevor Walsh reiterated a Buy rating on RKLB stock and lowered his price target to $90 from $130. Walsh said that he now expects the first launch in late Q2 2027. Consequently, he pushed the Neutron launch ramp by one year in his model.
Walsh views the delay as a “timing setback rather than a thesis change,” as the company is using this extended timeline to pull forward upgrades to Neutron. He is also bullish on the stock due to the pending acquisition of Iridium (IRDM) and continued demand for the Electron rocket.
Meanwhile, Morgan Stanley analyst Kristine Liwag reiterated a Buy rating on RKLB stock following the news of the delay, with a price target of $105. The 5-star analyst noted that management said the delay was due to a manufacturing-process issue, rather than a problem with the underlying composite material. While the delay is a setback, Liwag is encouraged that RKLB has addressed the problem using what it called its “highest-quality composite dome to date.”
Liwag updated her launch forecasts and now expects the debut flight in Q2 2027, followed by the first revenue-generating flight in Q4 2027. She now expects 3, 6, and 10 Neutron launches in 2028, 2029, and 2030, respectively.
