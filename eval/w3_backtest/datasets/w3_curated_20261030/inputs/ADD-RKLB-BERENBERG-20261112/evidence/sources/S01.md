# Berenberg raises Rocket Lab target

Publisher: StreetInsider
URL: https://www.streetinsider.com/Analyst+Comments/Rocket+Lab+USA+%28RKLB%29+PT+Raised+to+%2485+at+Berenberg/27328523.html
Published: 2026-11-12T02:37:00-05:00

## Relevant excerpt

Berenberg analyst Michael Filatov raised the price target on Rocket Lab USA (NASDAQ: RKLB) to $85.00 (from $83.00) while maintaining a Buy rating.
The analyst comments: “Q3 strengthened our core thesis that launch is the space industry’s bottleneck and that Rocket Lab is the best-placed listed company to benefit. Launch orders are running at c7x last year’s pace and, in our view, demand and pricing remain underappreciated. We believe there is further pricing upside across vehicles/missions, with Neutron already sold into the 2030s at a 27% higher base price.
Excluding the c$45m a quarter Rocket Lab spends on Neutron R&D, the existing business already earns a c10% adjusted EBITDA margin, and we expect significant operating leverage following first flight. We also expect Space Systems to reaccelerate into 2027 after a Q4 timing dip, and believe there is visibility that supports our above consensus 2027 Space Systems revenue estimate. We leave 2027E broadly unchanged and raise our 2028E pro-forma adjusted EBITDA by 8%.
