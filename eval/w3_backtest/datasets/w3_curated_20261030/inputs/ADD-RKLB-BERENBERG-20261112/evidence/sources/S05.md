# Berenberg initiates coverage

Publisher: TipRanks
URL: https://www.tipranks.com/news/rklb-vs-asts-why-berenberg-sees-more-upside-in-one-space-stock
Published: 2026-09-02

## Relevant excerpt

* Berenberg initiated Buy ratings on both RKLB and ASTS, with price targets of $83 and $92, respectively.
  * The analyst sees 32.7% upside for RKLB versus 64.9% for ASTS, making ASTS the higher-upside pick.
  * AST SpaceMobile’s 60+ mobile network operator partnerships and roughly 3 billion subscribers underpin its growth opportunity.
  * Rocket Lab’s key moat is its vertically integrated, end-to-end space capabilities.
Berenberg Bank analyst Michael Filatov initiated coverage of Rocket Lab USA (RKLB) and AST SpaceMobile (ASTS) with Buy ratings, citing significant long-term opportunities for both companies. However, he sees very different investment cases, with Rocket Lab offering diversified exposure to the space economy and AST SpaceMobile positioned for potentially rapid growth from its direct-to-smartphone service.
Filatov assigned Rocket Lab an $83 price target, implying 32.7% upside, while ASTS received a $92 price target, suggesting 64.9% upside.
## Rocket Lab: A More Diversified Space Play

Filatov describes Rocket Lab as “the only end-to-end public pure-play in space,” with operations spanning launch services, satellite manufacturing, components, and spectrum.
The analyst sees Rocket Lab’s established Electron launch business and upcoming Neutron medium-lift rocket as important growth drivers. According to him, launch capacity is becoming an industry bottleneck, with strong demand visible in Electron’s backlog and early bookings for Neutron.
Beyond launch, Rocket Lab’s satellite manufacturing and components businesses are benefiting from rising space budgets. The company’s planned Iridium (IRDM) acquisition could further expand its opportunity by adding globally harmonized spectrum and recurring applications revenue.

Filatov believes vertical integration is one of Rocket Lab’s biggest advantages, allowing the company to participate across multiple layers of the space ecosystem.
