# Oracle cloud multicloud strategy and data locality

- URL: https://www.itpro.com/cloud/public-cloud/oracle-leans-hard-on-one-size-fits-all-appeal-of-oci-for-enterprises
- Publisher: ITPro
- Published: 2026-04
- Role: Oracle客户与跨云

Oracle described making its database services available alongside AWS, Azure and Google Cloud offerings as a way to keep data near customers’ chosen AI pipelines with low-latency access. Its executive said customers sought access to services such as Vertex, Copilot, SageMaker and Bedrock. That broader multicloud strategy is separate from Snowflake’s proposed OCI service.
