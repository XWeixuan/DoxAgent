# Oracle PaaS and IaaS Universal Credits Service Descriptions

- URL: https://www.oracle.com/webfolder/dms/prod/d/Universal-Credits041025.pdf
- Publisher: Oracle
- Published: 2025-04-10
- Role: 既有第三方采购条款

The April 2025 Oracle service description specifies conditions for applying annual or monthly Universal Credit commitments toward third-party Marketplace offerings, including an aggregate 15% limit of the applicable commitment for qualifying customers in drawdown countries. The actual first-party Snowflake arrangement must be read under its own announced terms and applicable contract.
