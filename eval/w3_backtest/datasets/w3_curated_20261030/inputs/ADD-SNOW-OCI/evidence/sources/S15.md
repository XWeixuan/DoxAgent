# Snowflake FY2027 Q2 Reuters earnings report

- URL: https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/snowflake-lifts-annual-product-revenue-forecast-on-demand-for-cloud-data-platform-4886702
- Publisher: Reuters
- Published: 2026-09-02
- Role: 使用驱动与规模

Reuters reported Snowflake Q2 product revenue of $1.49 billion, 37% growth, and an increase in FY2027 product revenue guidance from $5.84 billion to $6.07 billion. The CEO said AI products contributed roughly half the growth acceleration. Those are Snowflake-wide metrics and do not quantify future OCI consumption.
