# Nebius Q2 2026 shareholder letter

The Q2 letter described four large contracts averaging over $1 billion in deal size, customer prepayments covering 50–60% of associated capex for applicable deals, and an estimated one year ten month payback period for Q2 deals. It raised year-end 2026 contracted power guidance to 5 GW and showed approximately $8 billion cash at quarter end. These are management metrics and guidance; contracted power is not operating compute capacity.
