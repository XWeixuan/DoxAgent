# Nebius Q2 2026 results and shareholder letter

- URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1513845/000110465926094568/tm2622968d1_ex99-1.htm
- Related letter: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1513845/000110465926094568/tm2622968d1_ex99-2.htm
- Publisher: Nebius / SEC
- Published: 2026-08-12
- Role: Financial scale, investment requirements and prepayment context.

Nebius reported second-quarter revenue of $582.3 million, up 454% from $105.1 million a year earlier; adjusted EBITDA was $236.2 million and continuing operations net loss $190.4 million. Second-quarter operating cash flow from continuing operations was $2.246 billion, while purchases of property, equipment and intangible assets totaled $5.657 billion. Its shareholder letter described about $8 billion cash at quarter-end, more than $9 billion expected 2026 customer prepayments, and a year-end contracted power target raised to 5 GW. Management said roughly 70% of Q2 deals included prepayments that covered 50–60% of associated capital expenditure; these were contract and management estimates, not a guarantee of subsequent performance.
