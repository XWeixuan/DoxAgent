# Nebius and Meta AI infrastructure agreement

- URL: https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-signs-new-ai-infrastructure-agreement-with-meta
- Publisher: Nebius
- Published: 2026-03-16
- Role: Prior customer commitment and capacity funding context.

Nebius announced a five-year agreement to supply Meta $12 billion of dedicated capacity across multiple locations, based on NVIDIA Vera Rubin, beginning in early 2027. Meta also committed to purchase available capacity across certain upcoming clusters up to $15 billion over five years; Nebius intended first to sell that capacity to third parties, with Meta purchasing remaining capacity. The company described the agreement as worth up to approximately $27 billion and left its 2026 guidance unchanged. The $15 billion component is conditional on available capacity and is not the same as the dedicated $12 billion component.
