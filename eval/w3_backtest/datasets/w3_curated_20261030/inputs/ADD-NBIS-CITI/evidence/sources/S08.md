# Commercial agreement with Microsoft

Nebius disclosed approximately $17.4 billion in contract value through 2031 subject to deployment and availability, with additional services potentially increasing it to approximately $19.4 billion. Following a grace period and failure to provide alternative capacity, Microsoft may terminate an affected GPU service for missed delivery dates. The agreement includes service levels and late-delivery damages; the company planned to finance associated capex with contract cash flow and secured debt.
