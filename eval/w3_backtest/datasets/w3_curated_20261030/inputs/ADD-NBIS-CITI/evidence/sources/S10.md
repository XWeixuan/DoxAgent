# CoreWeave closes $2.6 billion loan facility

- URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000357/ex991pr.htm
- Publisher: CoreWeave / SEC
- Published: 2026-08-10
- Role: 同业资产融资与条件

CoreWeave said it closed a $2.6 billion delayed-draw term loan to finance infrastructure dedicated to customer contracts, priced at SOFR plus 5.50%. The facility had approximately five-year maturity while underlying contracts averaged approximately three years, with renewal or re-leasing subject to agreement criteria. Its collateral and maturity differ from Nebius’s Citi facility.
