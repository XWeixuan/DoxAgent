# Nebius raises $775 million in first secured debt financing

- URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1513845/000110465926084452/tm2620683d1_ex99-1.htm
- Publisher: Nebius / SEC
- Published: 2026-07-17
- Role: 先前贷款结构与合同基线

Nebius described the earlier approximately $775 million loan as secured by deployed GPU infrastructure and cash flows from an investment-grade customer contract already in servicing. It said the loan plus those contract cash flows covered more than 100% of the underlying deployment capex. Separately it stated over $40 billion of additional investment-grade contracted revenue was in place and expressed an expectation of more asset-level financing.
