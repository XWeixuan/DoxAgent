# NVIDIA and Nebius partner to scale full-stack AI cloud

- URL: https://nebius.com/newsroom/nvidia-and-nebius-partner-to-scale-full-stack-ai-cloud
- Publisher: NVIDIA and Nebius
- Published: 2026-03-11
- Role: 既有股权资金与GPU供给

NVIDIA and Nebius announced a strategic partnership and a $2 billion NVIDIA investment. They described engineering collaboration and NVIDIA support for early adoption of its platforms, with a plan enabling Nebius to deploy more than five gigawatts of NVIDIA systems by the end of 2030. This is a plan and a separate funding source from debt.
