# Classiq and Sumitomo explore quantum Monte Carlo for finance

- URL: https://www.classiq.io/insights/classiq-powers-quantum-breakthrough-with-sumitomo-corporation-and-mizuho-dl-financial-technology
- Publisher: Classiq
- Published: 2025-03-25
- Role: 金融研究案例

Sumitomo used Classiq’s platform and algorithms from Mizuho-DL Financial Technology to evaluate future quantum applications in financial risk. The project compared two Monte Carlo approaches and addressed the trade-off between qubit use and circuit depth; Classiq reported 95% circuit compression. It was a feasibility project, not evidence of commercial quantum advantage in routine financial operations.
