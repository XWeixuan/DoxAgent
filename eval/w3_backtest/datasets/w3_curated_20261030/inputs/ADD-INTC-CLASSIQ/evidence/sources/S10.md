# Intel and QuTech Collaborate to Produce Silicon Qubits at Scale

- URL: https://download.intel.com/newsroom/archive/2025/en-us-2022-04-14-intel-and-qutech-collaborate-to-produce-silicon-qubits-at-scale.pdf
- Publisher: Intel
- Published: 2022-04-14
- Role: 芯片研究基础

Intel and QuTech reported a research manufacturing process producing more than 10,000 arrays containing several silicon spin qubits on one wafer with greater than 95% yield. The work was published in Nature Electronics and used 300mm manufacturing. This is an array-fabrication research result rather than a commercial fault-tolerant quantum computer.
