# Intel to Acquire Classiq to Create a Full-stack Quantum Computing Platform

- URL: https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/newsroom/news/corporate/intel-classiq-accelerate-scalable-quantum-computing.html
- Publisher: Intel
- Published: 2026-10-26
- Role: 收购原发

Intel announced a definitive agreement to acquire quantum software provider Classiq. Intel describes a proposed combination of Classiq’s automated algorithm synthesis with Intel’s silicon spin-qubit technology, manufacturing and quantum software, aiming to form a full-stack platform. Classiq’s synthesis is described as agnostic to qubit modality and hardware. The combination is proposed, not a completed acquisition.

Intel states that closing is subject to regulatory approvals and customary conditions and is expected in the first half of 2027. Until closing, the companies will operate independently. After closing Intel intends to maintain Classiq multi-platform integrations and hardware-agnostic software.
