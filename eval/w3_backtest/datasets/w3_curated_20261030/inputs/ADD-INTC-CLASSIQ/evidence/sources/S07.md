# Quantum Computing at the BMW Group

- URL: https://www.bmwgroup.com/en/news/allgemein/2025/quantum-computing.html
- Publisher: BMW Group
- Published: 2025-02-04
- Role: 客户应用与行业阶段

BMW says it works with Classiq and Nvidia on optimizing future vehicles’ electrical and mechanical architectures, including potential drivetrain and cooling design applications. BMW describes the collaboration as producing an innovative quantum application, while stating that practical industrial quantum computing remains in its infancy and requires more research.
