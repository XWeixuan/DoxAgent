# IBM and AMD partner on quantum computing

- URL: https://www.axios.com/2025/08/26/ibm-amd-quantum-computing
- Publisher: Axios
- Published: 2025-08-26
- Role: 竞争技术路线

IBM and AMD agreed to explore integration of AMD processors with IBM quantum systems to find algorithms beyond either approach alone. The account also reports a competing view that highly useful quantum computers could be decades away. This is a separate hardware/software research path and a timing perspective, not an Intel forecast.
