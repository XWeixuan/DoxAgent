# NTT DOCOMO BUSINESS and Classiq enter strategic business partnership

- URL: https://www.ntt.com/en/about-us/press-releases/news/article/2026/1008.html
- Publisher: NTT DOCOMO BUSINESS
- Published: 2026-10-08
- Role: 日本渠道与产品

Under a memorandum of understanding, NTT DOCOMO BUSINESS plans to propose and sell Classiq’s quantum software platform in Japan and work with Classiq to identify, develop and validate enterprise quantum use cases. Classiq supplies platform and algorithm/software expertise. The announcement describes a channel and planned validation, not quantified purchases or production revenue.
