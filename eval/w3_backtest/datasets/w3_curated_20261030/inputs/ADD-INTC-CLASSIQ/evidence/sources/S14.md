# Classiq and ParityQC partner on quantum execution

- URL: https://www.classiq.io/insights/classiq-and-parityqc-partner-to-optimize-and-streamline-quantum-execution
- Publisher: Classiq / ParityQC
- Published: 2026-07-15
- Role: 技术限制与伙伴

The companies announced plans to combine high-level algorithm synthesis with hardware-aware architecture for noisy devices and future fault-tolerant systems. Their work targets limited qubit connectivity, circuit complexity and costly swap operations. The announced collaboration concerns development and interoperability across evolving hardware, not a deployed commercial product result.
