# Intel Releases Quantum Software Development Kit Version 1.0

- URL: https://download.intel.com/newsroom/archive/2025/en-us-2023-02-28-intel-releases-quantum-software-development-kit-version-10-to-grow-developer-ecosystem.pdf
- Publisher: Intel
- Published: 2023-02-28
- Role: 既有软件平台

Intel released version 1.0 of its Quantum SDK after a 2022 beta. It supports quantum algorithm programming in simulation, including a generic simulator and a backend modeling Intel silicon spin qubits. Intel described interfaces with its hardware as part of its development path and said beta users were exploring fluid dynamics, astrophysics and materials design.
