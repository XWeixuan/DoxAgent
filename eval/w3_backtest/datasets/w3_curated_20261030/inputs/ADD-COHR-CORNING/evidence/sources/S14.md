# Lumentum Announces Fourth Quarter and Full Fiscal Year 2026 Results

- URL: https://investor.lumentum.com/financial-news-releases/news-details/2026/Lumentum-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Fiscal-Year-2026-Results/default.aspx
- Publisher: Lumentum
- Published: 2026-08-11
- Role: 光器件同业基线

Lumentum reported fiscal Q4 revenue of $1.01 billion and guided fiscal Q1 revenue to $1.225–1.275 billion. Management described optical circuit switching and its cloud module business as emerging growth drivers, including 1.6T adoption. Company risk disclosures include supply, capacity, customer order changes and product mix.
