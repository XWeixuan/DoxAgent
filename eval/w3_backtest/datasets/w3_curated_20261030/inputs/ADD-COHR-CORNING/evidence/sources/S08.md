# Corning Reports Third-Quarter 2026 Financial Results

- URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/24741/000002474126000273/glw-20261027xex99xq32026.htm
- Publisher: Corning / SEC
- Published: 2026-10-27
- Role: 原始分部结果

Corning Q3 Optical Communications net sales were $2,379 million, versus $2,072 million in Q2 and $1,652 million in Q3 2025. Enterprise Networks grew 84% year over year, while generative AI product sales grew over 100%. Company-wide core sales were $5.08 billion, core EPS $0.91; Q4 guidance was $5.2–5.3 billion in core sales and $0.96–1.00 in core EPS. Corning reported four long-term multibillion-dollar customer agreements in Q3, including Verizon and AT&T.
