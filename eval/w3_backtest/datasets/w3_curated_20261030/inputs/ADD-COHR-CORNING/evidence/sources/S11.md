# Verizon and Corning Announce Multi-year, Multi-billion Dollar Supply Agreement

- URL: https://www.corning.com/worldwide/en/about-us/news-events/news-releases/2026/09/verizon-and-corning-announce-multi-year-multi-billion-dollar-supply-agreement-for-broadband-expansion-and-next-gen-ai-infrastructure.html
- Publisher: Verizon and Corning
- Published: 2026-09-08
- Role: 既有光纤合同

Companies announced a multibillion-dollar agreement for over 80 million miles of high-density fiber and connectivity products from 2027 to 2032. The network is intended for broadband expansion and long-haul links serving AI hyperscalers. This is Corning fiber/connectivity supply, spanning multiple years and applications.
