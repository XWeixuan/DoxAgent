# Coherent Launches PhotonLink Integrated Optics Platform

- URL: https://ir.coherent.com/news-releases/news-release-details/coherent-launches-photonlinktm-integrated-optics-platform-ai
- Publisher: Coherent
- Published: 2026-09-21
- Role: 目标公司产品和商业进度

PhotonLink combines InP lasers, VCSEL arrays, silicon photonics, specialty fiber, optics and detectors for CPO, near-packaged optics and chip-to-chip connections. Coherent reported more than ten customer engagements each in CPO and NPO, anchor customers and long-term agreements, and expected platform revenue to begin ramping in calendar Q4 2026. The company described expansion of six-inch InP and specialty-fiber capacity.
