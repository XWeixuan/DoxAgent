# Corning Delivers Outstanding Third-Quarter Results

- URL: https://investor.corning.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2026/Corning-Delivers-Outstanding-Third-Quarter-Results1/default.aspx
- Publisher: Corning
- Published: 2026-10-27
- Role: peer primary results

Q3 core sales were $5.081 billion, up 19%, and core EPS $0.91. Optical Communications sales rose 44% to $2.379 billion and segment income rose 80% to $532 million. Enterprise Networks sales grew 84%, while generative AI product sales more than doubled. Q4 guidance was core sales $5.2 to $5.3 billion and core EPS $0.96 to $1.00. GAAP sales were $4.855 billion.

Optical segment sales were $2.379 billion versus $2.072 billion in Q2 and $1.652 billion a year earlier; segment income was $532 million versus $438 million and $295 million, respectively. Glass Innovations sales were $1.506 billion, down 3% year on year, and income was $328 million, down 10%. Corning said its Gen AI product growth outpaced GPU unit growth. These are Corning segment disclosures.
