# NVIDIA and Coherent Announce Strategic Partnership

- URL: https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-and-coherent-announce-strategic-partnership-to-develop-optics-technology-to-scale-next-generation-data-center-architecture
- Publisher: NVIDIA
- Published: 2026-03-02
- Role: issuer transmission

NVIDIA and Coherent announced a multibillion-dollar multiyear nonexclusive purchasing agreement and $2 billion NVIDIA investment, including work on advanced laser and optical technologies. Corning connectivity and Coherent photonic components are related but different products.
