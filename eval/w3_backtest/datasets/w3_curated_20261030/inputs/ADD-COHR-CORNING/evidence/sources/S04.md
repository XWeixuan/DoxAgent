# Coherent Corp. Reports Fourth Quarter and Full Year Fiscal 2026 Results

- URL: https://www.coherent.com/news/press-releases/fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2026-results
- Publisher: Coherent
- Published: 2026-08-12
- Role: issuer baseline

Fiscal Q4 revenue was $2.05 billion, up 34%, GAAP gross margin was 38.5%, non-GAAP gross margin 40.2%, and non-GAAP diluted EPS $1.74. Management described a shift from copper to optical datacenter connectivity and expanding capacity.
