# Coherent FY2026 Annual Report

- URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/820318/000110465926114851/tm264290d2_ars.pdf
- Publisher: Coherent / SEC
- Published: 2026-10
- Role: issuer exposure and constraints

Coherent describes two reportable segments, Datacenter and Communications and Industrial. It attributes stronger transceiver shipments to hyperscaler AI infrastructure demand, and elevated demand for ZR/ZR+ and conventional telecom transport. It is expanding indium phosphide capacity in Sherman, Texas amid an industry-wide shortage. NVIDIA made a $2 billion equity investment and agreed to a nonexclusive multibillion-dollar purchase commitment; the separate capacity agreement may require equipment, labor and working-capital investments through 2030. This issuer-specific exposure does not make Corning fiber/connectivity revenue equivalent to Coherent transceiver revenue.
