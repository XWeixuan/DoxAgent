# Corning and Meta Announce Multiyear, up to $6 Billion Agreement

- URL: https://investor.corning.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2026/Corning-and-Meta-Announce-Multiyear-up-to-6-Billion-Agreement-to-Accelerate-US-Data-Center-Buildout/
- Publisher: Corning and Meta
- Published: 2026-01-27
- Role: 既有数据中心合同

Corning and Meta announced a multiyear agreement of up to $6 billion for optical fiber, cable and connectivity solutions for US data centers. Corning said Meta would anchor an expansion of optical cable manufacturing in Hickory, North Carolina. The amount is an agreement ceiling over multiple years.
