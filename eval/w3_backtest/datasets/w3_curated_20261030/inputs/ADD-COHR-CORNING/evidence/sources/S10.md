# NVIDIA Announces Strategic Partnership With Lumentum

- URL: https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-strategic-partnership-with-lumentum-to-develop-state-of-the-art-optics-technology
- Publisher: NVIDIA
- Published: 2026-03-02
- Role: 同业竞争与供给

NVIDIA announced a nonexclusive multiyear agreement with Lumentum including a multibillion-dollar purchase commitment and future capacity access for advanced laser components. NVIDIA also announced a $2 billion investment to support R&D, capacity and US manufacturing. This is a separate supplier agreement.
