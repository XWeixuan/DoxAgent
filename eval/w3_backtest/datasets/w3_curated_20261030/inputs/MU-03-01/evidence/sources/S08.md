# HBM supply constraints persist

Publisher: TrendForce
URL: https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20260929-13255.html
Published: 2026-09-29T16:03:54+08:00
Role: event disclosure

TrendForce raised its 2027 blended HBM ASP forecast to 121% year on year, citing constrained wafer capacity, higher-priced HBM4 product mix and gradual HBM4E ramp in the second half of 2027. GPU and ASIC manufacturers were considering 8-Hi rather than 12-Hi to reduce stack and system cost and support more devices with available bits. A base die is still needed per stack, so TrendForce forecast 8-Hi per-gigabit prices 10–20% above 12-Hi. These figures are forecasts; product mix affects blended ASP.
