# 4Q26 memory price forecast

Publisher: TrendForce
URL: https://www.trendforce.com/research/download/RP260924PL
Published: 2026-09-24
Role: prior research bulletin

TrendForce’s September 24 report page outlines 4Q forecasts: server and HBM receive priority capacity, CSPs increase RDIMM orders, and long-term agreements constrain price rises. The table of contents describes 23–28% QoQ enterprise SSD increases and 15–20% overall NAND increases, while consumer categories face cost pressure. These are projected contract price movements, not realized sales.
