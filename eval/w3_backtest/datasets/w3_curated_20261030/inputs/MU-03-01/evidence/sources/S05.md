# Micron reports fiscal Q3 2026 results

Publisher: Micron
URL: https://micron.gcs-web.com/index.php/news-releases/news-release-details/micron-technology-inc-reports-record-results-third-quarter
Published: 2026-06-24
Role: company baseline

Micron reported $41.46 billion revenue for fiscal Q3. Cloud Memory revenue was $13.769 billion and Core Data Center $11.524 billion. Its fiscal Q4 outlook was $50 billion plus or minus $1 billion, about 86% gross margin and $31.00 non-GAAP diluted EPS plus or minus $1. HBM4 was shipping in high volume for its lead platform, with qualification samples to multiple end-customers; HBM4E volume production was expected in calendar 2027. These are reported results, shipments and company guidance, respectively.
