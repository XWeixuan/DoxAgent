# Diverging DRAM and NAND 2027 outlook

Publisher: TrendForce
URL: https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20260730-13158.html
Published: 2026-07-30
Role: prior supply and consumer conditions

TrendForce anticipated DRAM supply would remain tight in 2027 amid HBM allocation and server demand, while NAND supply could loosen in the second half as capacity arrives and consumer demand remains weak. It expected substantial new fab output only in 2028, and noted high memory prices could affect CSP procurement and investment plans.
