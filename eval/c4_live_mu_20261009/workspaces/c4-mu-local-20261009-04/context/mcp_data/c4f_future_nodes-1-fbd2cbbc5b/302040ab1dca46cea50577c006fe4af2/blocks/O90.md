Financial performance by business unit

Now turning to quarterly financial performance by business unit.

Cloud Memory Business Unit (CMBU) revenue was a record $16.3 billion and represented 30% of total

company revenue. CMBU revenue was up 18% sequentially, driven by higher pricing and bit shipments.

CMBU gross margins were 83%, flat sequentially, driven by higher pricing, offset by higher HBM mix.
