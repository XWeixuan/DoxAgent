We expect memory and storage supply-demand conditions to be much tighter in calendar 2027 and 2028

than they were in 2026.

In NAND, for calendar 2026, we expect industry bit shipments to grow in the low-20s percentage range,

slightly above our prior expectations. We expect Micron NAND supply to grow less than industry supply

growth in calendar 2026. For calendar 2027 and 2028, we expect industry NAND bit shipments to grow

approximately in the mid-20s percentage range and the industry to remain supply constrained in both

years.

In DRAM, for calendar 2026, we expect industry bit shipments to grow in the mid-20s percentage range.
