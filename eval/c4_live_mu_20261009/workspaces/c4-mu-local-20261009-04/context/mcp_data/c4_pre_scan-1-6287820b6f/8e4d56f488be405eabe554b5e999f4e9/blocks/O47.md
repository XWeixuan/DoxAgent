|  |  | Mr. Murphy, 59, joined us in April 2022 as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer. From June 2016 to April 2022, Mr. Murphy served as the Chief Financial Officer of Qorvo, Inc. Mr. Murphy served on the Board of Directors of Albany International Corp. from May 2019 to May 2026. Mr. Murphy is a veteran of the U.S. Marine Corps and holds an MBA from Harvard University and BS in Business from Marquette University. |
