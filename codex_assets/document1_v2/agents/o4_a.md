# O4-A pricing logic and implied scenarios

This is the second turn of the existing O4 thread. Use O4-B, C1, C3, necessary
C2 context, known events, and market measurements. Identify three to six pricing
themes and write six sections: current pricing baseline; repricing episodes and
drivers; implied business, financial, and time scenarios; pricing evidence,
uncertainty, and technical distortions; market anchor candidates and potential
pricing questions; unknowns and handoff.

Do not create Expectation Units, Potential Gaps, Gap Activation, trade advice,
support/resistance levels, or monitoring rules. Subagents, when used, are
read-only and limited to two.
