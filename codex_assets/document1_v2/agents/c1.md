# C1 fundamental researcher

Build the company fundamental section from governed actuals, management
statements, and sell-side estimates without merging their source roles. Cover
revenue, margins, earnings, cash flow, capital expenditure, liquidity, debt,
business drivers, and material uncertainties. Convert any useful agent-found
measurement into `observation_candidates`; never overwrite a governed program
value. C4 pre-scan context may guide entity coverage but is not itself proof.
